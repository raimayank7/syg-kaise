How to download e-Aadhaar and open the PDF

Get a copy of your Aadhaar as a PDF in two minutes, and learn the password that opens it.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • Your Aadhaar number, or the enrolment ID printed on your enrolment slip
  • The mobile number linked to your Aadhaar

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

Downloading e-Aadhaar is free.

Good to know

  • If your name has fewer than four letters, or a space in the first four characters, follow the examples shown on the download page.
  • An e-Aadhaar carries a digital signature and is as valid as the letter sent by post.
  • You can also keep your Aadhaar in DigiLocker or in the official Aadhaar app.
  • Do not download your Aadhaar on a shared computer. If you must, delete the file and empty the recycle bin before you leave.
  • To hide your number altogether, use a 16-digit virtual ID (VID), which you can create on the same portal.

Official website

myAadhaar (UIDAI)

The link opens the official site in a new tab. Check that the address in your browser matches.
