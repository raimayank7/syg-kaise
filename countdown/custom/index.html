Countdown

Make your own countdown

Type a name and a date for a wedding, exam, trip, birthday or launch. You get a live timer and a link to share.

How it works

  1. Enter what you are counting to and pick the date. Add a time if the moment matters, such as an exam start.
  2. The timer starts at once and updates every second.
  3. Copy the link or send it on WhatsApp. The name, date and time are in the link itself, so whoever opens it sees the same countdown, in their own time zone.

Good uses

  • Weddings and engagements: share one link with all the guests.
  • Exam dates: keep the tab open on a study desk.
  • Trips and visas: see the days left to pack and book.
  • Product launches and deadlines at work.

Privacy

Nothing is saved on our side. The details live only in the link. Anyone who has the link can read the name and date in it, so do not put private information in the name.

Ideas for names

Keep the name short and clear: "Wedding day", "Board exam starts", "Goa trip", "Launch day". If friends in other cities will open the link, add the city or time zone to the name. Remember that anyone with the link can read the name, so avoid private details. To stop a countdown, simply stop sharing the link; nothing is stored on our side.

Time zones

The timer uses the clock on your own device. If you send the link to someone in another country, the same moment shows as the same number of seconds left, but the printed date and time are in their local time. For an online event across countries, state the time zone in the name, for example "Webinar 6 pm IST".
