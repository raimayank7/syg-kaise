About Teachers' Day (India)

Teachers' Day is observed in India on 5 September. A Press Information Bureau explainer of 5 September 2024 says the day honours Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, born on 5 September 1888, and that in the mid-1960s 5 September became the fixed date for the National Teachers' Awards function because of his birthday.

It is on the same calendar date every year, so only the weekday changes.

Over the 8 years from 2026 to 2033, Teachers' Day (India) falls on a weekend 3 times, so on those years most people get it free of work or school without a weekday off. It lands on a Friday or Monday in 2031, 2033, which gives a long weekend. Where it falls on a Sunday, check whether your office or board gives the next day off, as practice differs.

Making the most of an awareness day

Awareness days are useful only if something follows. Pick one small action that fits your group: a talk, a quiz, a drive, a donation, a poster. Announce it two weeks before so people can join, and prepare materials a week before.

Take two or three photos and write a few lines afterwards. A simple record helps you do better next year and shows what the group achieved.

Ideas for the days before

  • Write a letter to a teacher who changed your path.
  • Organise a short programme in class.
  • Make a card by hand.
  • Contact an old teacher by phone.

Common questions

What is the date of Teachers' Day (India) next year?

Sunday, 5 September 2027.

Does the timer use my time zone?

Yes. It counts to midnight at the start of the day on your device's clock, so a visitor in another country sees a different number of hours.

Why is the date different from last year?

It is on the same calendar date every year, so only the weekday changes. The weekday in the table above shows how it shifts.
