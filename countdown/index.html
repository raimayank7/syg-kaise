What you will find here

Each page counts down to the next date of one occasion and then lists its date for the next eight years, with the weekday, so you can plan leave, travel and events well ahead. The year pages (2027, 2028 and more) list every occasion in order with a note on which ones give a long weekend.

Lunar festivals are not listed

Diwali, Holi, Eid and other festivals that follow lunar calendars change date each year and are set by an almanac or an announcement. We leave them out rather than guess. Use the official holiday list of your state or office.
