Reading this list

Every date in this table follows the Gregorian calendar or a weekday rule such as "second Sunday of May", so it can be worked out in advance. Lunar festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Navratri) are left out because their dates depend on the almanac; use the official holiday list of your state or office for those. Weekdays matter: an occasion on a weekend gives no extra day off, while one on a Friday or Monday makes a long weekend.

Long weekends and weekends in 2033

Falling on a Friday or Monday: Valentine's Day, Earth Day, Memorial Day (USA), National Doctors' Day (India), Independence Day (USA), Independence Day (India), Teachers' Day (India), Labor Day (USA), Halloween, Children's Day (India), Black Friday.

Falling on a Saturday or Sunday: New Year's Day, Easter, Labour Day (May Day), Mother's Day, World Environment Day, Father's Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas), Christmas, New Year's Eve.
