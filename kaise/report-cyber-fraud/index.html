How to report online or UPI fraud

Lost money to an online fraud? Call 1930 at once, tell your bank, and file a complaint on the national cybercrime portal.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • The date, time and amount of the transaction
  • The transaction or UTR number (in the SMS or in your UPI app)
  • Your bank or wallet name and account number
  • Screenshots of the messages, the app screen and the fraudster's number or UPI ID

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

Reporting is free, on the helpline and on the portal.

Good to know

  • You never need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to RECEIVE money. A PIN is only for sending money.
  • Never share an OTP, PIN, CVV or password. No bank, courier, electricity office or police officer asks for them.
  • Do not install a screen-sharing app because a caller asks you to. It lets the caller see and control your phone.
  • Under Reserve Bank of India rules, a customer who reports an unauthorised transaction to the bank within three working days has the strongest protection. So report at once.
  • Do not believe anyone who promises to recover your lost money for a fee. That is a second fraud.
  • Suspicious calls and messages, even if you lost nothing, can be reported on the Chakshu facility of the Sanchar Saathi portal.

Official website

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

The link opens the official site in a new tab. Check that the address in your browser matches.
