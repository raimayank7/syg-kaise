What to do when your phone is lost or stolen

Block the SIM, report to the police, and block the handset on the government's CEIR service so that nobody can use it.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • The IMEI number of the phone (printed on the box and the bill; a dual-SIM phone has two)
  • The mobile numbers that were in the phone
  • A copy of the police report
  • An identity proof, and the purchase bill if you have it
  • Another mobile number for the OTP

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

Blocking a phone on CEIR is free.

Good to know

  • Find and note your IMEI today: dial *#06# on your phone, or look on the box. Keep it somewhere other than the phone.
  • Turn on "Find My Device" (Android) or "Find My" (iPhone) now. With it you can locate, lock or erase a lost phone.
  • A blocked phone can still be traced when someone puts a SIM in it; the police are alerted. Do not go to recover it yourself.
  • If you see a transaction you did not make, call the cyber fraud helpline 1930 at once.
  • Use a screen lock, and a separate lock for payment apps.

Official website

CEIR, Sanchar Saathi (Department of Telecommunications)

The link opens the official site in a new tab. Check that the address in your browser matches.
