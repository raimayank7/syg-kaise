How to file a consumer complaint

A faulty product, a refund not given, a service not delivered: complain to the company, then the helpline 1915, then the consumer commission.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • The bill, invoice or order number
  • Proof of payment
  • Photos or videos of the defect
  • Copies of your messages and emails to the seller, with dates
  • The seller's or company's name and address

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

The helpline is free. In a consumer commission the fee depends on the value of the goods or services; under the rules in force when we checked, there is no fee where that value is up to ₹5 lakh.

Good to know

  • A complaint must normally be filed within two years of the problem arising.
  • A District Commission hears cases where the value of the goods or services paid for is up to ₹50 lakh. Larger cases go to the State or National Commission.
  • You can argue your own case. A lawyer is not compulsory.
  • Ask for a clear, reasonable relief: the refund, the repair, and a fair amount for your trouble and costs.
  • For banks, insurance, telecom and electricity there are also separate ombudsmen or grievance forums, which are often quicker. The helpline can tell you which one applies.
