How to use DigiLocker for your documents

Keep your Aadhaar, driving licence, vehicle RC and marksheets on your phone in the government's DigiLocker.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • A smartphone or computer
  • Your mobile number
  • Your Aadhaar number, with a mobile number linked to it

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

DigiLocker is free.

Good to know

  • Documents under "Issued Documents" come straight from the issuing department and are treated as equal to the originals under the Information Technology rules for DigiLocker.
  • A driving licence and vehicle RC shown in DigiLocker or in the mParivahan app are accepted by traffic police, as the transport ministry has advised.
  • Files that you scan and put in "Uploaded Documents" are only your own copies. They do not have the same legal standing.
  • Never tell anyone your DigiLocker PIN or OTP. With them a person can see all your documents.
  • Not every board, university and department has joined yet. If your document is not found, the issuer may not be on DigiLocker.

DigiLocker

The link opens the official site in a new tab. Check that the address in your browser matches.
