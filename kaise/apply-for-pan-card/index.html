How to get a PAN card: free instant e-PAN

Get a free e-PAN in minutes using Aadhaar, or apply for a printed PAN card through the authorised agencies.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • An Aadhaar with your full date of birth and a linked mobile number
  • You must be an adult who has never been allotted a PAN
  • For a printed card: a photograph, signature and the fee

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

Instant e-PAN on the income tax portal is free. For a printed card delivered in India, the agencies' fee was reported as about ₹107 when we checked; confirm it on the agency's site.

Good to know

  • An e-PAN is a valid PAN. You can use it everywhere a PAN is asked for.
  • From 1 April 2026 new PAN application forms came into use in place of the old Form 49A. The agency website shows the current form; you do not need to find it yourself.
  • One person can hold only one PAN. If you already have one and lost the card, ask for a reprint. Do not apply for a new PAN.
  • The name and date of birth on PAN come from your Aadhaar. Correct any mistake in Aadhaar before you apply.
  • Beware of websites with "pan" in their name that are not the official agencies. They charge several times the real fee.
