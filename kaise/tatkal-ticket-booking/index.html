How to book a Tatkal train ticket on IRCTC

What Tatkal is, when booking opens, and how to prepare so that you can book in the first minute.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • An IRCTC account that is verified with your Aadhaar
  • The IRCTC Rail Connect app or the website irctc.co.in
  • Names and ages of the passengers saved in the "Master List"
  • A fast way to pay, such as UPI

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

A Tatkal ticket costs the normal fare plus a Tatkal charge, which depends on the class and the distance. The exact fare is shown before you pay.

Good to know

  • At most four passengers can be booked on one Tatkal ticket.
  • There is no refund for cancelling a confirmed Tatkal ticket. Book only when you are sure of the journey.
  • No concessions apply on Tatkal tickets.
  • Use mobile data or a steady Wi-Fi connection, and do not press the back button during payment.
  • Tickets from touts and unauthorised agents can be cancelled, and you can be fined. Book from your own account or at the station counter.
  • Railway booking rules are changed from time to time. Check the notices on the IRCTC site before an important journey.
