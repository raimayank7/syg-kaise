How to use a guide

  1. Open the guide for your task.
  2. Collect everything in the "What you need" list first.
  3. Open the official website from the link in the guide and follow the steps one by one, ticking them off.
  4. Write down the reference number you get at the end.

Common questions

Is this a government website?

No. It is an independent information site. Every guide links to the real official website, where the application is actually made.

Will you fill my form for me?

No. We do not fill applications, accept documents or take money. Anyone doing so in our name is a fraud.

How current is the information?

All guides were checked on 11 November 2026. Fees and rules change, so always confirm on the official website before you pay.

Why Hindi and English?

Many official portals are easier to follow in your own language. Each guide has a button to switch between the two.
