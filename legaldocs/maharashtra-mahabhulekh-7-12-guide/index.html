Maharashtra 7/12 extract: what Mahabhulekh offers

Maharashtra's land record website Mahabhulekh lets you view a 7/12 extract, 8A extract and property card. This page describes what its home page shows.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source

Source: the official Mahabhulekh v2.0 portal page at bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in, opened in a browser on 13 November 2026. We read the home page text and its menu links only; we did not search any record. Portals change their menus, so confirm on the portal itself.

What the portal says it is

The page describes itself as "MAHA Bhulekh (i.e. Maharashtra Bhumi Abhilekh) - a land record website of Maharashtra state (India) that provides 7/12 extract, 8A extract and Property Card online to citizens". Its title reads "Gaon Namuna Number 7/12, 8A, Property Card and K-Prat" in Marathi.

A notice you must know

The page states, in Marathi, that the Bhulekh website is available only in Marathi and that use of third-party transliteration tools for English is not expected. Expect to enter place names as the portal shows them in Marathi.

The choices on the page

  • A question: "Do you know your 11 digit Property UID number?" with Yes and No.
  • Select Record of Right: 7/12, 8A, Property Card or K-Prat.
  • For 7/12 details: choose the district, then the taluka and village, then the survey or gat number (with options such as survey number and part numbers).
  • The district list on the page covers the districts of Maharashtra shown in its drop-down.

How to use it carefully

  • Know the district, taluka, village and survey or gat number from your document before you start.
  • Match the name and number on the portal to your sale deed or mutation entry.
  • An online extract is for viewing; if you need a certified copy or a signed one, ask the Talathi or tehsil office what is accepted.
  • We state no fee or stamp amounts.

Two more notices, read in the archived copy of 18 July 2026

The Internet Archive copy of the Mahabhulekh page, dated 18 July 2026, carries a notice in Marathi that the information shown on this site cannot be used for any government or legal purpose (our translation). Read it as the portal’s own warning: for a legal purpose, ask the office which certified copy it needs.

The same copy carries a second notice. It says that if the online 7/12 you see differs from the handwritten 7/12 in the total area, the unit of area, the account holder’s name or the account holder’s area, you can send an application for correction online to the Talathi through the e-Hakka system. It names the address bhumiabhilekh.maharashtra.gov.in, says to register and log in as shown under the option "Mutation 7/12", and to upload the old handwritten 7/12 with the online application.

The form on that copy asks for district, taluka, village, survey or gat number (with a choice between survey number and lettered survey number, and a part 1 field), a mobile number, a language for the result and a captcha. The list of languages has 24 choices, from Marathi and English to Santhali, Sindhi, Kashmiri and Sanskrit. Its district list has 35 entries, ending with Solapur.

Common questions

Is there an English version of Mahabhulekh?

The portal's own notice says the site is available only in Marathi. Check the portal for any change.
