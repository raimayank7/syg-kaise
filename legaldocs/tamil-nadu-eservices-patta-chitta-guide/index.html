Tamil Nadu patta, chitta and A-register: what the e-Services portal offers

Tamil Nadu’s land records portal is called E-Services of Land Records. This page lists what its home page showed, in our own English, as archived on 31 March 2025.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source and its date

Source: the official page eservices.tn.gov.in/eservicesnew/home.html of the Commissionerate of Land Survey and Land Settlement, Government of Tamil Nadu, read through the Internet Archive copy of 31 March 2025 in a browser on 13 November 2026. The portal itself showed "Last revised: Mar 15, 2025" on that copy. When we opened a deeper page of the same site directly on 13 November 2026, it answered "INVALID ACCESS TO THE SITE! Login from Home Page", so we describe the home page only, not any search screen. The page is in Tamil with an English switch; the English wording below is ours, and no native Tamil speaker has checked it.

Notices on the page

  • The land details on the portal are in Unicode Tamil, so the portal asks users not to use the browser’s translate option because it may mistranslate names.
  • The portal says the information is updated and published immediately, so there is no need to visit any revenue office to get it.
  • Applications for patta mutation (patta maaruthal) can be given at any common service centre in the state, where a fee of Rs 60 is collected, according to the page on that date.
  • The page says the field sketch (pulappadam) copies can be downloaded free of charge through this electronic service.

Services listed on the home page

  • Apply for patta mutation.
  • View patta, chitta and field sketch details (rural or natham).
  • View A-register details (rural or natham).
  • Verify patta and chitta details (rural or natham).
  • View details of government poramboke land.
  • View field sketch details (rural or natham).
  • View a patta copy.
  • Town survey register and town survey map.
  • Application status.
  • Field boundary map or report.
  • Related links: department circulars and amendments to circulars, land transfer, a fee details page (government order) with a view link.

What the common terms mean

We have not read the portal’s own definitions on this page, so we do not define patta, chitta, A-register or natham here; ask the village administrative office or a lawyer. The Haryana and Punjab guides on this site quote the short descriptions those portals give for their own terms.

How to use it carefully

  • Start from the portal address above, not from an advertisement or a message link, and never pay anyone who offers to "get" a record for you without looking at the portal yourself.
  • Have the district, taluk or tehsil, village and survey or plot number ready before you start.
  • What the screen shows is for checking. For a legal purpose, ask the office or a lawyer which certified copy is needed.
  • The fee, the common service centre route and the "free download" statement are the portal’s own words on the archived date; check them on the portal before you pay anything.

Common questions

Is the Rs 60 fee still correct?

It is what the portal page showed on 31 March 2025 for giving a patta mutation application at a common service centre. Check the portal and the fee details link on it for the current amount.
