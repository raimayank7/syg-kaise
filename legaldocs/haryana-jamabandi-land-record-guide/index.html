Haryana land records (jamabandi): what the portal offers

Haryana publishes land records on its Revenue Department jamabandi website. This page describes what that website says and offers.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source

Source: the official Haryana jamabandi portal page at jamabandi.nic.in, opened in a browser on 13 November 2026. We read the home page text and its menu links only; we did not search any record. Portals change their menus, so confirm on the portal itself.

What the portal says about itself

The site calls itself "An Official Web site to get Haryana Land Record Documents". Its About text says the Revenue Department, Haryana is using information technology to update land records and that the site helps people access official land records such as registration, mutation and jamabandi. It says people can view details of sanctioned mutations and the jamabandi of any land in Haryana using the owner name, khewat number and khasra numbers. It also says the site provides information about property registration and the documents needed for it, and has a knowledge base on revenue terminology. A news line on the page says WebHalris has been implemented in all 143 tehsils of the state.

The portal's own definitions

Jamabandi: "a document prepared as part of record-of-right in every revenue estate". The portal says it contains entries about ownership, cultivation and up-to-date of various rights in land, and is revised every five years when a jamabandi is prepared by the Patwari and attested by a Revenue Officer, with two copies prepared. Mutation Register: the portal says mutation indicates the changes to be brought about in ownership and title of land, and that it contains information about the khewat of the last jamabandi proposed to be corrected and the information proposed to be established. (We quote only the short descriptions on the home page; the portal has longer pages.)

Services listed in its menus

  • Jamabandi nakal for checking, and a way to get a verifiable copy of nakal (a login page).
  • Query pages for owner details, kashtkar details, makbuja details, total land and irrigation details.
  • Urban and rural property records search.
  • Encumbrance certificate: a procedure document, an "Apply New" page and a "View Status" page.
  • Property registration: deed registration appointment, checklist for deed registration, a deed registration SOP, deed templates, collector rates, and a page on stamp duty and registration fees (we state no amounts).
  • View stay orders; view cadastral maps; dashboards for deeds and mutations; a server status check.

Encumbrance certificate: the portal's own steps

The portal links a one-page document titled "Procedure to obtain Encumbrance Certificate", which we downloaded from jamabandi.nic.in on 13 November 2026 and read in full. Its six steps are: 1. Open Jamabandi.nic.in. 2. Click on Property Registration > Encumbrance Certificate > Apply new. 3. Enter mobile number and OTP. 4. Enter user details. 5. Add property details and upload the nakal. 6. After the application is approved, the Encumbrance Certificate can be downloaded. The portal also links a "Deed Registration SOP" PDF, but that file is an image with no readable text, so we did not read it, and a "Checklist for Deed Registration" page that we did not open.

How to use it carefully

  • Start from the portal address above, not from a search-engine advertisement or a message link.
  • Have the district, tehsil, village, and owner name or khewat and khasra numbers ready, as the portal says it searches by owner name, khewat number and khasra numbers.
  • A nakal on the screen is for checking; the portal offers a separate route for a verifiable copy. Ask the office which one your purpose needs.
  • Do not share an OTP or password with anyone who offers to "get your record".

Common questions

Is this the official site?

The address we opened is jamabandi.nic.in, which calls itself the official Haryana land record website. Confirm the address in your own browser and check the Haryana government site for links to it.
