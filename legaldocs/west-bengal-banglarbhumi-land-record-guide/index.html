West Bengal BanglarBhumi: what the portal offers

West Bengal's land department runs BanglarBhumi. This page lists what the home page showed us on the day we read it.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source

Source: the official BanglarBhumi home page at banglarbhumi.gov.in, opened in a browser on 13 November 2026. We read the page text only; we did not search any record. The page loaded slowly; we waited about 15 seconds before it showed its text. Portals change their menus, so confirm on the portal itself.

What the home page lists

  • Top menu: About Us, Land Reforms, GEM Branch, M & M Branch, Requisition Branch, Agricultural & Census Branch, Survey & Settlement Branch, Establishment, Law Branch, ARTI, LMTC, Rent Controller, Thika Tenancy.
  • Service links: Mouza Information, Public Grievance, Query Search, Know Your Property, Citizen Services, and Revenue Court Case Management System (RCCMS).
  • Sign In and Sign Up links.
  • Support links: FAQ, Help, Forum, Terms Of Use, Privacy Policy, Linking Policy, Feedback and a contact address.

Notices on the page that day

The page carried an excerpt of an order of the Chief Secretary dated 18 May 2026 on domestically made products and sustainable consumption, a state relief fund appeal, and a note that the IFMS server including the GRIPS portal would be unavailable from 9 PM on Friday 4 September 2026 to Sunday 6 September 2026, during which BanglarBhumi payments and GRN search might be unavailable. It also said that conversion of land under the EKWMA Act 2006 requires sanction of the competent authority under section 9 of that Act. These are the portal's own words; we have not read the Act.

Who runs it and when it was last updated

The footer names the Director of Land Records and Survey, 35 Survey Building, Gopal Nagar Road, Kolkata-700027, and says contents are provided by the Land and Land Reforms and Refugee Relief and Rehabilitation Department, Government of West Bengal. It shows "Site Last Updated on 20/09/2024", which is the portal's own statement and older than the notices above.

How to use it carefully

  • Start from the portal address above, not from an advertisement or a message link, and never pay anyone who offers to "get" a record for you without looking at the portal yourself.
  • Have the district, taluk or tehsil, village and survey or plot number ready before you start.
  • What the screen shows is for checking. For a legal purpose, ask the office or a lawyer which certified copy is needed.
  • If you need to pay a fee through the portal, use the payment option on the portal itself and check the notice about maintenance windows first.

Common questions

Is this the official site?

The address we opened is banglarbhumi.gov.in, which names the West Bengal Land and Land Reforms Department. Confirm the address in your own browser.
