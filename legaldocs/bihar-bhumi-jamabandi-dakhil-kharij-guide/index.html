Bihar Bhumi: jamabandi, dakhil-kharij and land services

Bihar's Revenue and Land Reforms Department runs BiharBhumi. The portal is in Hindi with an English switch; below is our own English wording of its menu.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source

Source: the official BiharBhumi home page at biharbhumi.bihar.gov.in, opened in a browser on 13 November 2026. We read the page text only; we did not search any record. We read the Hindi page; the English renderings below are ours, so check them against the portal. Portals change their menus, so confirm on the portal itself.

Dakhil-kharij (mutation) services in the menu

  • Apply online for dakhil-kharij (mutation).
  • Check dakhil-kharij errors; see application status, including applications returned by the Circle Officer.
  • A general notice page, filing an objection in a dakhil-kharij case, and the cause list of the Circle Officer's court.
  • Dakhil-kharij of government land, and a form for dakhil-kharij together with registration.

Records and maps

  • View the jamabandi register, including a version in all 22 languages of India.
  • Land map (bhu-manchitra) and delivery of revenue maps to your door.
  • The Directorate of Land Records and Survey and the Bihar Land Tribunal.
  • A link to Kaithi script material and a Kaithi document translation service.

Other services listed

  • Apply online for an LPC (the menu says "LPC"; we have not checked what the abbreviation stands for on the portal) and see its application status.
  • Land rent (bhu-lagan).
  • Parimarjan Plus (correction) application and its status.
  • E-measurement (e-mapi), the Revenue Court Management System, land acquisition online reporting portal, a public grievance portal, and Aadhaar or mobile seeding status.
  • Help and questions-and-answers, video training material, a glossary of revenue terms and a Revenue Maha-Abhiyan page.

How to use it carefully

  • Start from the portal address above, not from an advertisement or a message link, and never pay anyone who offers to "get" a record for you without looking at the portal yourself.
  • Have the district, taluk or tehsil, village and survey or plot number ready before you start.
  • What the screen shows is for checking. For a legal purpose, ask the office or a lawyer which certified copy is needed.
  • If a menu item is not clear in Hindi, use the portal's English switch or ask the Circle office rather than guessing.
