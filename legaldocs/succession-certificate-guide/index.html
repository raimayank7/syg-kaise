Succession certificate: what the Act says

A succession certificate lets the holder collect debts and securities owed to a deceased person. Part X of the Indian Succession Act sets the procedure.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 13 November 2026: the Indian Succession Act, 1925 (India Code copy, with amendments noted up to 20 December 2025).

We read sections 370 (opening), 371, 372, 373 (opening) and 381. Other sections of the Part were not read. This is a guide to what the Act says, not advice for your case.

Which court: section 371

The District Judge within whose jurisdiction the deceased ordinarily resided at the time of death may grant the certificate; if he had no fixed residence, the District Judge within whose jurisdiction any part of the property of the deceased may be found.

Limits: section 370

Section 370(1) says a succession certificate shall not be granted with respect to any debt or security to which a right is required by section 212 to be established by letters of administration (with a proviso about an Indian Christian). For the purposes of the Part, "security" includes promissory notes, debentures, stock and other securities of the Central or a State Government, and other items listed in the section; read the list.

The application: section 372

Section 372(1) says the application is made to the District Judge by a petition signed and verified in the manner the Code of Civil Procedure prescribes for a plaint, setting out: (a) the time of death; (b) the ordinary residence of the deceased at death and, if outside the Judge's jurisdiction, the property within it; (c) the family or other near relatives and their residences; (d) the right in which the petitioner claims; (e) the absence of any impediment under section 370 or any other provision; and (f) the debts and securities for which the certificate is sought. Under 372(2), a person who verifies a petition containing a statement he knows or believes to be false, or does not believe true, shall be deemed to have committed an offence under the provision of the Indian Penal Code named in the text we read; check how that reference now reads after the 2023 criminal-law changes.

Hearing: section 373

If the District Judge is satisfied there are grounds for entertaining the application, he fixes a day for hearing and causes notice to be served on any person to whom special notice should be given and to be posted on a conspicuous part of the court-house and published in such other manner as the Judge thinks fit, subject to High Court rules; on that day he decides the right to the certificate in a summary manner. We read only the opening of the section.

Effect: section 381

Subject to the Part, the certificate is conclusive as against persons owing the specified debts or liable on the securities, and affords full indemnity to such persons for payments made or dealings had in good faith with the holder, even if section 370 was contravened or there was another defect.

Common questions

Do I need a succession certificate for land?

The sections we read speak of debts and securities. Land and mutation are handled under other laws and state procedures that this site does not cover.
