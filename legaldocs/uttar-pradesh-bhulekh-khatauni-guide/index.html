Uttar Pradesh Bhulekh (khatauni): what the portal offers

Uttar Pradesh publishes land records on UP Bhulekh, run by the Board of Revenue. This page describes what its home page shows (the page text is in Hindi).

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source

Source: the official UP Bhulekh portal page at upbhulekh.gov.in, opened in a browser on 13 November 2026. We read the home page text and its menu links only; we did not search any record. Portals change their menus, so confirm on the portal itself. The page loaded after a short wait and its text is in Hindi; the English below is our translation.

What the portal says about itself

The portal says it was started by the Board of Revenue (Rajasva Parishad), Uttar Pradesh; that citizens of the state can get all land-related information on it; and that data for all land in the state is available. It says citizens can view records about their land, such as status (whether a plot, by its gata or unique code, is under dispute, has been sold, its map, and the mutation register), by clicking on the gata number. The footer says the information is owned, updated and maintained by the Board of Revenue and that the National Informatics Centre state unit gives technical help.

Services under "Bhulekh Sevayen"

  • View a copy (nakal) of the khatauni (record of rights).
  • Get a certified copy of the khatauni by online payment.
  • Know the sale status of a plot or gata.
  • Know whether a plot or gata is under litigation.

Logins and related portals

The page offers a khatauni login, a real-time khatauni login and a Khasra portal (ekhasra.up.gov.in). It also lists lists of district, tehsil, pargana and land type, a "search government land" link, a complaint registration and complaint status link (Jansunwai), and links to stamp and registration (igrsup.gov.in), revenue cases (vaad.up.nic.in), DILRMP, e-District and other government portals. We opened none of these deeper pages.

How to use it carefully

  • Open the portal by typing the address; the page offers a security guidelines document from the state.
  • Have your district, tehsil, village and gata number ready, as the portal speaks of clicking on the gata number.
  • For a certified copy, use the portal's own online payment route and ask the tehsil what it accepts for your purpose.
  • We state no fees.

Common questions

Is there a way to complain about a wrong entry?

The home page lists complaint registration and status links to the state Jansunwai portal. We did not open them; ask the tehsil office as well.
