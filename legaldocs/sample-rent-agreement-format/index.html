Rent agreement format

A written lease of a house, flat or shop for a stated term and rent.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

RENT AGREEMENT This rent agreement is made at [Place] on [Date] between: [Lessor's name], resident of [Lessor's address] (the Lessor); and [Lessee's name], resident of [Lessee's address] (the Lessee). 1. The Lessor lets to the Lessee the premises at [Full address of premises] (the Premises) for use as [Residence or shop or other use]. 2. The term is [Term] starting on [Start date]. 3. The rent is Rs [Amount] per [Month or year], payable by [Day or date] of each period by [Mode of payment]. 4. The Lessee has paid Rs [Amount] as a refundable security deposit, to be returned when the Lessee hands back the Premises in the condition received, less any proven damage. 5. The Lessee will use the Premises only for the stated use, will not sublet without written permission, and will pay [Electricity, water and other charges] as agreed. 6. Either party may end this agreement by giving [Notice period] notice in writing. 7. On the end of the agreement the Lessee will return possession of the Premises to the Lessor. What this sample is for

A written lease of a house, flat or shop for a stated term and rent.

What the law says (sections read on 13 November 2026)

  • Section 105 of the Transfer of Property Act defines a lease as a transfer of a right to enjoy immovable property for a certain time, express or implied, or in perpetuity, in consideration of a price paid or promised or of rent.
  • Section 107: a lease from year to year, for a term exceeding one year, or reserving a yearly rent can be made only by a registered instrument; where a lease is by a registered instrument, it is executed by both lessor and lessee.
  • Registration Act section 17(1)(d) lists such leases among documents that shall be registered; section 18(c) lists shorter leases among those that may be registered.

Check before you sign

  • Check the term and the way rent is stated against section 107 before deciding whether the agreement must be registered.
  • Ask the Sub-Registrar about registration and stamp duty for your state; this site states none.
  • Write down the meter readings and the condition of the premises in a signed list.

Stamp paper, registration and fees

This site states no stamp duty, registration fee or state rule. They differ by state and change. Ask the Sub-Registrar, the state stamp office or a lawyer. Sections 3 and 35 of the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 deal with instruments chargeable with duty and with unstamped instruments; see our stamp paper page.

Common questions

Is this sample enough on its own?

No. It is a general sample. Registration, stamp paper, witnesses and local rules depend on the document and your state. Ask the Sub-Registrar or a lawyer before you sign.
