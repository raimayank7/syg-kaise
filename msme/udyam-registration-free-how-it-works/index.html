Udyam registration: what the official portal says

Udyam registration is how an MSME gets its permanent number and certificate. This page restates what the official portal page said when it was archived on 16 March 2026.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 13 November 2026: the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 (India Code copy, read through an Internet Archive snapshot of 20 April 2024), and the Udyam Registration portal page on registration, as kept by the Internet Archive on 16 March 2026. We read the Act as in that copy and the portal page as archived; amendments and later portal changes were not read, so check the Udyam portal and India Code before you rely on a figure.

Only the government portal registers you

The portal says that except this portal of the Government of India and the Government’s Single Window Systems, no other private online or offline system, service, agency or person is authorised or entitled to do MSME registration or undertake any activity related to the process. The page title also says registration is zero cost, with no fee. If someone asks you for money to “do” Udyam registration, the portal says nobody is entitled to.

What the portal says you get

  • A permanent registration number after registration; the enterprise is called an “Udyam” and the process “Udyam Registration”.
  • An online certificate with a dynamic QR code, from which the web page on the portal and the details about the enterprise can be reached.
  • No renewal: “There will be no need for renewal of Registration.”
  • Help from the single-window systems at the Champions Control Rooms and at the District Industries Centres (DICs).

How you register

The portal says the process is fully online, paperless and based on self-declaration, with no documents or proof required to be uploaded. The Aadhaar number is required: of the proprietor for a proprietorship firm, of the managing partner for a partnership firm, and of a karta for a Hindu Undivided Family. For a company, a Limited Liability Partnership, a cooperative society, a society or a trust, the organisation or its authorised signatory provides its GSTIN and PAN along with its Aadhaar number. PAN and GST linked details on investment and turnover are taken automatically from government databases.

PAN and GSTIN

The portal says that having PAN and GSTIN (as applicable under the CGST Act, 2017 and as notified by the Ministry of MSME in S.O. 1055(E) dated 5 March 2021) is required for Udyam registration with effect from 1 April 2021. It says any deficiency of information for earlier years when the enterprise had no PAN may be filled on self-declaration.

One registration only

No enterprise shall file more than one Udyam registration, but any number of activities, manufacturing or service or both, may be specified or added in the one registration.

Old registrations

The page says enterprises registered under EM-II or UAM, or any other registration issued by an authority under the Ministry of MSME, had to register again on or after 1 July 2020, and that registrations made before 30 June 2020 stayed valid only until 31 December 2021.

False information

Whoever intentionally misrepresents or attempts to suppress the self-declared facts and figures in the Udyam registration or updation process is liable to the penalty specified under section 27 of the Act, according to the portal. We have not read that section.

Common questions

Does Udyam registration cost anything?

The portal page says registration is free and that no costs or fees are to be paid to anyone.

Do I upload documents?

The portal page says no documents or proof are required to be uploaded, and that only the Aadhaar number is needed (with PAN and GSTIN where required).

When do I renew it?

The portal page says there is no need for renewal of the registration.
