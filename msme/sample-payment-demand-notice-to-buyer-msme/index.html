Payment demand notice to a buyer for delayed payment (sample)

A written demand to the buyer that records the delivery date, the invoice and the agreed days, before a reference to the Facilitation Council.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

To, [Name of the buyer] [Address] Date: [Date] Subject: Payment due for supplies — invoice no. [Invoice number] dated [Invoice date] — Rs [Amount] Sir/Madam, 1. I/We are a micro/small enterprise registered under Udyam, registration number [Udyam registration number], and supplier of [Goods or services] to you. 2. The goods were delivered / services were rendered on [Date of delivery or completion] against your order [Order number and date]. You did not object in writing to the acceptance within fifteen days. [Or: Your written objection dated [Date] was removed on [Date].] 3. [Choose one: We agreed in writing that payment would be made within [Number] days (not more than forty-five). / There is no written agreement on the payment period.] The amount of Rs [Amount] has not been paid as of the date of this letter. 4. Under section 15 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 payment was due on or before [Due date], and under section 16 you are liable to pay compound interest with monthly rests at three times the bank rate notified by the Reserve Bank from [Date from which interest runs]. 5. Please pay Rs [Amount] with the interest due within [Number] days of this letter. Failing that, I/we will make a reference to the Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council under section 18 of the Act. Copies of the invoice, delivery challan or completion record and the order are enclosed. Yours faithfully, ______________________ [Name and designation] [Enterprise name and address] [Phone or e-mail] Enclosures: [List them]

What this sample is for

A written demand to the buyer that records the delivery date, the invoice and the agreed days, before a reference to the Facilitation Council.

Which parts of the Act it follows

  • Section 15: payment on or before the agreed date, never more than forty-five days from acceptance or deemed acceptance; otherwise before the appointed day.
  • Section 2(b): the appointed day is the day after fifteen days from acceptance or deemed acceptance.
  • Section 16: compound interest with monthly rests at three times the bank rate notified by the Reserve Bank, from the appointed day or the day after the agreed date.
  • Section 18: a reference to the Facilitation Council; decision within ninety days.

Check before you send

  • Fill the dates from your own invoice, challan and written agreement.
  • Delete the option of paragraph 3 that does not apply.
  • Do not state a rate: use our calculator with the current bank rate if you want to include a figure.
  • Send by registered post or courier with proof and keep a copy. This is not a legal notice drafted by a lawyer; ask a lawyer if the amount is large.
