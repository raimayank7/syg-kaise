Telangana minimum wages from 1 June 2026

Telangana has fixed a new basic minimum wage per month under the Code on Wages, 2019, in place of all earlier notices under the Minimum Wages Act. This page restates the order, which we read in full.

Read on 14 November 2026: the Government of Telangana Labour, Employment, Training and Factories (Lab-I) Department G.O.Ms.No.6 dated 30 May 2026, with the notification to be published in the Extraordinary Telangana State Gazette of 1 June 2026, signed by the Secretary to Government; we read pages 1 to 4 of the 27-page file, in English, from the images. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the order says

The order says the Government, after the Commissioner of Labour’s recommendations, the Telangana State Minimum Wages Advisory Board’s recommendations and a Cabinet Sub-Committee’s revised recommendations, orders the fixation of the basic minimum rates of wages per month for employees in all the establishments defined under the Code on Wages, 2019, in supersession of all earlier notifications issued under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, in the State of Telangana, with effect from 1 June 2026. It adds that this is subject to the outcome of pending writ petitions and contempt cases before the Hon’ble High Court for the State of Telangana or any other court cases pending in the matter.

The notification is made under sections 6 to 8 of the Code on Wages, 2019 and section 6 of the General Clauses Act, 1897.

The three zones and the covered employments

Zone I is municipal corporations, Zone II is municipalities, and Zone III is rural and other areas. The rates apply to all the employments in Schedule I and Schedule II and to the skill designations in Schedule III of the order. We did not read those schedules, so we cannot list the employments or the designations in each skill category.

The skill categories

The order defines the unskilled as personnel doing operations primarily reliant on physical or manual effort that need no prior experience; the semi-skilled as those who apply skill gained by experience on the job under the supervision or guidance of a skilled employee, including supervision over unskilled occupation; the skilled as those whose operations involve skill and competence gained through experience on the job or through training as an apprentice in a technical or vocational institute; and the highly skilled as those whose operations need a specific level of perfection and competence acquired through intensive technical or professional training or practical experience over a considerable period, with full responsibility for judgment or decision.

Cost of living allowance

The order says the basic rates are linked to the Consumer Price Index numbers at 443 points (base year 2001 = 100 series). The Commissioner of Labour shall notify the cost of living allowance every six months, on 1 April and 1 October, taking into account the average rise in the State Industrial Workers or Agricultural Workers Consumer Price Index for the half year ending December and June respectively. For every rise of one point over 443 points, an increase of Rs 33.86 per point per month shall be paid as variable dearness allowance. We have not seen any notification of the allowance, so the table shows the basic rates only.

Conditions in the order

  • To get the day wage, the monthly wage is divided by 26 days, which includes the rest day wages.
  • A trainee is paid 75 per cent of the wage of the category, but not less than the minimum wage of an unskilled worker.
  • Employers pay uniform and equal minimum rates to male, female, transgender and physically challenged employees who do identical or equivalent work.
  • Where an existing notified wage is higher than the recommended wage, an additional 10 per cent over the existing wage is to be provided; any category already receiving a higher wage than those notified continues to be paid it.
  • Work on a designated weekly rest day or a public holiday is paid at double the normal rate, subject to the applicable labour rules.
  • In the final calculation of pay, fractions are rounded off to the nearest 50 paise.
  • Wages are to be deposited directly in the worker’s bank account by bank cheque or direct electronic transfer (NEFT, RTGS or IMPS).
  • Work beyond the standard 8 hours a day is overtime paid at double the standard rate of wages.
  • For a piece-rated employee, the wage for 8 hours’ work shall not be less than the minimum wage of the skill category, and the piece rate is calculated proportionately.
  • Where workers are engaged through a contractor or service provider, the occupier or principal employer is responsible for payment of the minimum wages.

Piece-rated work and new employments

The order says the minimum wages for piece-rated and labour-intensive operations (power looms, garments, spinning mills, brick kilns, tobacco including beedi, and handloom weaving) will be fixed by a separate committee, so no rate for them is in this table. It also adds ten new employments to Schedule I, among them e-commerce and courier service, offices of religious and social institutions, amusement parks, mobile tower maintenance and TV cable networks, handicrafts, cane and bamboo, borewell drilling, ready mix concrete, aluminium and tin products, and LPG storage and distribution agencies.

What we do not state

We did not read Schedules I to III, any cost of living allowance notification, the Telangana rules under the Code, or the court cases the order mentions. The order itself is subject to those cases, so rates may change. Check the Telangana Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in a municipal corporation area?

That is Zone I: Rs 16,000 a month basic from 1 June 2026, before any cost of living allowance, in G.O.Ms.No.6.

Is there a dearness allowance on top?

The order says it is payable above 443 index points at Rs 33.86 per point per month, with the Commissioner of Labour to notify it every six months. We have not seen that notice.

Are these rates final?

The order says it is subject to the outcome of pending writ petitions and other court cases in the High Court.
