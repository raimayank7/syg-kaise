Bihar minimum wages from 1 October 2026

Bihar has fixed new minimum wages under the Code on Wages (Bihar) Rules, 2026. This page restates the notification, which we read in Hindi, with the limits of the copy we had.

Read on 14 November 2026: the Government of Bihar Labour Resources and Migrant Workers Welfare Department notification (adhisuchna) fixing minimum rates of wages for workers from 1 October 2026 under section 6 of the Code on Wages, 2019 read with rule 5 of the Code on Wages (Bihar) Rules, 2026; the copy we read is in Hindi and has its S.O. number and date left blank, and we read Schedules I and II from the page images. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

How the notification builds the rate

The notification says the Governor of Bihar, using the power in section 6 of the Code on Wages, 2019 read with rule 5 of the Code on Wages (Bihar) Rules, 2026, fixes the minimum wage rates for the workers to whom the Code applies. Schedule I is a calculation table of the consumer price index for the months January to June 2026; Schedule II gives the rates for four categories of worker; Schedule III gives piece rates for 12 employments.

Schedule I lists the All India Consumer Price Index (2016 = 100) for January to June 2026 as 148.6, 148.5, 149.1, 149.9, 150.8 and 151.9, multiplies each by the link factor 4.63 x 4.93 x 2.88 to restate it on the 1960 = 100 base, and totals the six figures to 59,085.82. Schedule II then shows, for each category, the rate effective from 1 September 2022, the rate applied before 1 October 2026, the increase in rupees, and the new rate: unskilled 436 + 7 = 443, semi-skilled 452 + 7 = 459, skilled 551 + 9 = 560, highly skilled 672 + 10 = 682 rupees a day.

Notes in the notification

  • The rates come into force on 1 October 2026 under the Code on Wages, 2019 (note 1).
  • The rates have been worked out on an ad hoc basis from the average All India Consumer Price Index; after the State Advisory Board is constituted under the Code on Wages, 2019, its ex post approval will be obtained (note 2).
  • The daily rate is converted to a monthly rate by multiplying by 26, and the monthly to the daily rate by dividing by 26 (note 3).
  • The rates include the pay for the weekly rest day within a seven-day period (note 4).
  • For work on the weekly rest day or any other day beyond the normal hours, the worker is entitled to wages according to rules 6 and 7 of the Code on Wages (Bihar) Rules, 2026 (note 5).
  • Men and women get the same wage for the same work or work of a similar nature (note 6).

Piece rates

Schedule III gives piece rates for twelve employments, beginning with stone breaking and stone crushing (for example, for taking stone out of the mine and carrying it by bullock cart, the rate per 100 cubic feet is Rs 958 within a quarter mile, up from Rs 943). We did not read the rest of Schedule III, and we do not restate it here.

What the copy we read does not give

The copy has the S.O. number and the date of the notification left blank, so we cannot give them. The Hindi text speaks of the “proposed” rates in note 4, and note 2 says the State Advisory Board’s approval is still to be obtained, so the rates may be revised. We did not read the Code on Wages (Bihar) Rules, 2026, the earlier Bihar notification of 1 September 2022, or any area classification, and the schedule shows no zones. Check the Bihar Labour Resources Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Bihar?

Rs 443 a day from 1 October 2026 in Schedule II of the notification we read; Rs 11,518 for a month of 26 days, which we worked out as note 3 directs.

Is this the final notified rate?

The copy we read has a blank date and S.O. number, and says the rates were worked out on an ad hoc basis pending the State Advisory Board’s approval. Check the Labour Department for the final text.

Are there different rates for cities and villages?

The schedule we read shows one daily rate per category and no zones.
