Himachal Pradesh minimum wages from 1 April 2025 (two employments)

Himachal Pradesh issues a separate notification for each scheduled employment. This page restates two of the nineteen in the notification of 24 June 2025 and says what we did not read.

Read on 14 November 2026: the Government of Himachal Pradesh Department of Labour, Employment and O.P. notification No. Shram(A)4-1/2024 dated Shimla-2, 24 June 2025, the authoritative English text under clause (3) of article 348 of the Constitution; we used its text for the agriculture and the road and building construction employments. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the notification says

The notification says the Governor of Himachal Pradesh was of the opinion that the minimum rates of wages in the scheduled employments may be revised with effect from 1 April 2025. It recalls that a Minimum Wages Advisory Committee, constituted by notification No. Shram (A) 4-2/2018-P-II dated 24 April 2023, met on 4 April 2025 and approved an increase of the minimum wages payable to all the categories of workers in all 19 scheduled employments, applicable from 1 April 2025. Each employment then has its own notification under sub-section (2) of section 5 of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.

For agriculture, the notification revises the minimum wage of the unskilled workers to Rs 425 a day, or Rs 12,750 a month, from 1 April 2025. For construction or maintenance of roads or building operations it prints Rs 425 for unskilled workers, Rs 452 for semi-skilled, Rs 493 for skilled, Rs 588 for highly skilled, and Rs 493 for clerical and non-technical supervisory staff, each with its monthly figure in the table above.

Notes for agriculture

  • No distinction between the minimum wages of male or female and adult or non-adult for the same and similar nature of work.
  • The wages of apprentices are regulated under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
  • Where work is done on piece work basis, the wages shall not be less than the time rate for the category.
  • A 25 per cent increase applies over and above the minimum wages in the Scheduled Tribes Areas in Himachal Pradesh.

Notes for road and building construction

  • The same rules on male and female, adult and non-adult, apprentices and piece work as above.
  • A worker in a category not specifically mentioned is not to be paid less than the minimum rates for the similar category having the same skill.
  • A 20 per cent increase is admissible over and above the minimum rates for workers working inside tunnels.
  • A 25 per cent increase applies over and above the minimum rates in different categories of workers on Under Construction Hydro-Electric Power Projects in non-tribal areas.
  • A 25 per cent increase applies over and above the minimum wages in the Scheduled Tribal Areas in Himachal Pradesh; for workers on Under Construction Hydro-Electric Power Projects in tribal areas an additional 10 per cent increase applies.

The skill categories

The notification defines an unskilled employee as one who does operations involving simple duties, requiring little or no independent judgment or previous experience, although familiarity with the occupational environment is necessary. It lists the jobs under each category for each employment; we have not copied those lists. A highly skilled worker is one capable of working efficiently and supervising efficiently the work of skilled employees.

What we do not state

The notification has one notification for each of 19 scheduled employments, including public motor transport, shops and commercial establishments, forestry, chemicals, engineering, tea plantation, manufacturing establishments, hotels and restaurants, and private educational institutes. We transcribed only the two above, so we state nothing for the other 17. The notification is from June 2025 and the Minimum Wages Advisory Committee meets again, so a later revision may exist; we did not find one. We did not read the Code on Wages, 2019 or any Himachal rules under it. Check the Himachal Pradesh Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled agricultural worker in Himachal Pradesh?

Rs 425 a day, or Rs 12,750 a month, from 1 April 2025, in notification No. Shram(A)4-1/2024 of 24 June 2025. A later revision may exist.

Is there an extra for tribal areas?

Yes: the notes say a 25 per cent increase applies over and above the minimum wages in the Scheduled Tribes Areas in Himachal Pradesh.

How many employments does the notification cover?

Nineteen scheduled employments, each with its own notification; this page restates only agriculture and road and building construction.
