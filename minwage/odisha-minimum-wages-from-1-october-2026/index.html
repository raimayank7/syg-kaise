Odisha minimum wages from 1 October 2026

Odisha revises the variable dearness allowance on its minimum wage every six months. This page restates the Labour Commissioner’s notice for 1 October 2026, which we read in Odia.

Read on 14 November 2026: the notice (bijnapti) of the Labour Commissioner, Odisha, Bhubaneswar, announcing the increase in variable dearness allowance from 1 October 2026; it is in Odia, the handwritten date and number on our copy are hard to read, and we read the text and the table from the scan. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the notice says

The notice recalls that by notification No. 5308 of 18 July 2024 of the Odisha Government’s Labour and Employees’ State Insurance Department, published in the Odisha Gazette, the minimum daily wages for unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled and highly skilled workers in the State were fixed again. It says that, under that notification, the variable dearness allowance (VDA) is fixed again twice a year, on 1 April and 1 October, at the rate of Rs 2.60 for each point of rise in the All India Consumer Price Index for industrial workers (base 2016 = 100), and notified by the Labour Commissioner.

It then says that in the past half year the average index rose from 147.50 to 149.80, an increase of 2.30 points, so the VDA has to be increased for the half-year starting 1 October 2026. The Labour Commissioner therefore notifies that all workers in all the scheduled employments, in the four categories, get an additional Rs 6 a day over the VDA payable before 1 October 2026, and the minimum daily wage with VDA from that date is as in the table.

What we do not state

The notice gives daily rates only; it states no monthly figure, no zone and no rate for any particular employment, so we state none. The notice is in Odia, no native speaker has checked our summary of it, and we checked each figure against the stated increase of Rs 6. The handwritten number and date on our copy are not clear enough to quote. We did not read the notification of 18 July 2024, the earlier April 2026 notice, the Code on Wages, 2019 or the Odisha rules. The allowance is revised every six months, so a notice for 1 April 2027 will replace this one. Check the Odisha Labour Commissioner before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Odisha?

Rs 478 a day including variable dearness allowance from 1 October 2026: Rs 472 before, plus Rs 6, per the Labour Commissioner’s notice.

How is the Rs 6 worked out?

The notice says the VDA is Rs 2.60 for each point of rise in the consumer price index, which rose by 2.30 points (147.50 to 149.80), giving the Rs 6 a day increase.
