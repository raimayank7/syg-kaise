Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu minimum wages from 1 October 2026

This Union Territory fixes a basic daily wage and adds a special allowance that it revises from time to time. This page restates the declaration of 4 November 2026, which we read in full.

Source

Read on 14 November 2026: the UT Administration of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Department of Labour and Employment (Daman), declaration of special allowance under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, No. LE/LI/DMN/MWA-3(II)/2026/437 dated 4 November 2026, signed by the Commissioner-cum-Secretary (Labour); read from the scan. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the declaration says

The declaration says the basic pay was fixed in the Union Territory in respect of all the scheduled employments under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 by notification No. LE/LI/DMN/MWA-3(II)/2023/349 dated 18 August 2023, and that the special allowance in addition to the basic pay was fixed by the notifications dated 28 October 2024 (No. 2024/507), 9 May 2025 (No. 2025/88), 25 November 2025 (No. 2025/1085) and 30 April 2026 (No. 2026/70).

The daily rate of special allowance, payable on the basis of the cost-of-living index number from 1 October 2026, is increased at the rate of Rs 12.00 a day in respect of all the employments in the Union Territory under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948. The minimum rates of wages payable in respect of all the employments in the schedule to the Act in the Union Territory are then as in the table.

What we do not state

The declaration gives daily rates for three categories, with no highly skilled category, no monthly figure and no rate for particular employments, so we state none. We did not read the earlier notifications it cites, the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 as it now applies there, or any rule under the Code on Wages. The special allowance is revised from time to time, so a later declaration will replace this one. Check the Labour Department of the Union Territory before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Daman and Diu or Dadra and Nagar Haveli?

Rs 513.50 a day (basic Rs 441 plus special allowance Rs 72.50) from 1 October 2026, in the declaration of 4 November 2026.

By how much did the special allowance rise?

The declaration says it is increased at the rate of Rs 12.00 a day from 1 October 2026.
