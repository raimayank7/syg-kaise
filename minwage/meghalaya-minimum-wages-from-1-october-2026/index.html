Meghalaya minimum wages from 1 October 2026

Meghalaya revises its minimum wage and variable dearness allowance every six months. This page restates the notification of 8 October 2026, which we read in full.

Minimum wage plus revised VDA, per day

Category of workerMinimum wage (Rs a day) + revised VDA (Rs a day) = total (Rs a day), from 1 October 2026
Unskilled525 + 39 = 564
Semi-skilled565 + 43 = 608
Skilled605 + 45 = 650
Highly skilled645 + 48 = 693

Source

Read on 14 November 2026: the Government of Meghalaya Department of Labour, Employment and Skill Development notification No. LE&SD.9/2023/Pt/33 dated Shillong, 8 October 2026, issued under section 6 of the Code on Wages, 2019 and signed by the Principal Secretary; read from the scan. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the notification says

The notification says the Governor of Meghalaya, in exercise of the powers in section 6 of the Code on Wages, 2019, revises the rates of minimum wages, inclusive of variable dearness allowance (VDA), for all employees doing skilled, semi-skilled or unskilled, manual, operational, supervisory, managerial, administrative, technical or clerical work for hire or reward, across the skill categories in the State, as shown in the table. The rates are effective from 1 October 2026 and supersede the department’s earlier notification No. LE&SD.9/2023/Pt/26 dated 1 May 2026. It says it issues with the concurrence of the Finance (PR) Department, I/D No. F(PR)-86/2026 dated 16 September 2026.

The four categories, as the notification defines them

  • Unskilled: an occupation that in its performance requires the application of simply the operating experience and involves no further skills.
  • Semi-skilled: an occupation that requires the application of skill gained by experience on the job, capable of being applied under the supervision or guidance of a skilled employee, and includes supervision over the unskilled occupations.
  • Skilled: an occupation that involves skill and competence through experience on the job or through training as an apprentice in a technical or vocational institute, and the performance of which calls for initiating and judgement.
  • Highly skilled: an occupation that calls for a specific level of perfection and competence acquired through intensive technical or professional training or practical occupational experience for a considerable period, and that also requires an employee to assume full responsibility for judgement or decision.

Other terms in the notification

  • The revised rates are inclusive of variable dearness allowance but exclusive of other concessions, if any, enjoyed by the employees.
  • The existing hours of work, 8 hours a day and 48 hours a week, continue until further orders.
  • The minimum rates include the wages payable for the weekly day of rest.
  • The rate of wages for overtime work shall be double the ordinary rate of wages.
  • The rates are revised every six months by adopting a Consumer Price Index (CPI) linked variable dearness allowance, rounded off to the next higher rupee.

What we do not state

The notification gives daily rates only, with no monthly figure, no zones and no rate for particular employments, so we state none. We did not read the earlier notification of 1 May 2026, the Code on Wages, 2019 or the Meghalaya rules. The allowance is revised every six months, so a later notification will replace this one. Check the Meghalaya Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Meghalaya?

Rs 564 a day (Rs 525 plus Rs 39 variable dearness allowance) from 1 October 2026, in the notification of 8 October 2026.

Do the rates include the rest day?

Yes: the notification says the minimum rates include the wages payable for the weekly day of rest.

What is the overtime rate?

The notification says overtime work shall be paid at double the ordinary rate of wages.
