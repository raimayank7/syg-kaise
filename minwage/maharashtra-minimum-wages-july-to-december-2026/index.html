Maharashtra minimum wages, 1 July to 31 December 2026

Maharashtra declares a special allowance twice a year and adds it to a zone-wise basic rate. This page shows ten employments from the Commissioner of Labour’s notice of 5 August 2026, with the limits of what we read.

Source

Read on 14 November 2026: the notice of the Office of the Commissioner of Labour, Mumbai, dated 5 August 2026, No. RuralDevelopment/MWRules/Special Allowance/2026(2)/office-10/7997, announcing the special allowance and total minimum wages for 1 July to 31 December 2026; the original is a scanned Marathi table and our figures come from an English translation of it, which we did not check against the Marathi. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

How the notice builds the rate

The English translation says that under the Minimum Wages Rules, 1948 the amount of basic minimum wage plus the amount of special allowance equals the total minimum wage for workers in the scheduled employments, announced for the period 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2026. The basic rates are listed zone-wise, per month in rupees, for skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled workers. The special allowance is one fixed amount per month for an employment, the same in every zone, and it is added to each zone’s basic rate. Each cell above reads basic + special allowance = total.

Which industries are shown

The notice covers more than sixty scheduled employments, including cashew, bakery, cement, paper, oil mills, tobacco and brick-making, with their own layouts: some have two zones, some have per-kilogram or per-thousand rates, and some have extra categories. We show ten whose rows show three zone columns in the same layout, and we checked that basic plus special allowance equals the printed total in every cell. We state nothing for the others. Read the notice for any employment not shown here.

What we do not know

The page we read does not say which towns or areas are Zone 1, Zone 2 and Zone 3; the zone lists are in earlier Maharashtra notifications that we did not read. We also did not read the Marathi original: the figures come from an English translation made by a compliance firm, so a mistake in translation or typing is possible. The Commissioner of Labour’s office issues the notice and should be asked if a figure matters.

What we do not state

We did not read the Minimum Wages Rules, the Code on Wages, 2019 as applied in Maharashtra, the shops and establishments rules, or any figure for the second half of the year. The special allowance is revised every six months, so a notice for a later period will replace this one. Check the Maharashtra Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

Which zone is my town in?

The notice we read does not list towns by zone. Ask the Commissioner of Labour or read the earlier notification that defines the zones.

Is the special allowance different in each zone?

In the rows we show, the special allowance is a single amount for an employment, and the zone changes only the basic rate.
