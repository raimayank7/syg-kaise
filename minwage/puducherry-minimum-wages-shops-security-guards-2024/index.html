Puducherry minimum wages, September 2024: shops and security guards

Puducherry publishes one notification for each scheduled employment, with a dearness allowance on top. This page restates two of the nineteen in the Gazette of 20 September 2024.

The daily figures are our division of the monthly rate by 26, as the notification’s note directs; the notification itself prints only monthly rates. These are the basic minimum rates: dearness allowance is added on top, as the section below explains.

Source

Read on 14 November 2026: the Gazette of Puducherry, Part I, Extraordinary, No. 125 of Friday 20 September 2024, which prints Labour Department notifications dated 2 September 2024, among them G.O. Ms. No. 09/AIL/Lab./G/2024 (Shops and Establishments) and No. 10/AIL/Lab./G/2024 (Security Guards); we read the Gazette’s text and checked the shops table against the page images. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the notifications say

The Shops and Establishments notification says the Lieutenant-Governor of Puducherry, after publishing the proposal, considering the objections and consulting the Minimum Wages Advisory Board, which recommended an increase, revises the minimum rates of wages payable to employees in shops and establishments in the Union Territory, in supersession of the notification of 22 August 2016 (G.O. Ms. No. 09/AIL/Lab./G/2016). The Security Guards notification likewise supersedes the notification of 24 February 2015. Both are made under sections 3, 5 and 13 of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, and come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette of Puducherry.

Dearness allowance on top

For shops and establishments the notification says the dearness allowance is linked to the average of the Puducherry City Consumer Price Index for the year 2023, which is 426 points (base 2001 = 100), and for every rise of one point above 426 an increase of Rs 32.40 per point per month is paid as dearness allowance. For security guards it is linked to the 2021 average of 366 points, with Rs 45.20 per point per month for every point above 366. In both, the allowance is calculated on 1 April every year on the average of the indices for the preceding twelve months, January to December, and the first calculation is effective from the date of publication, based on the previous year’s average. We have not seen the Puducherry Labour Department’s later notices of the allowance, so we state no allowance figure.

Other notes

  • To arrive at the daily rate, the monthly rate is divided by 26 (note 2).
  • Where the nature of work is the same, there is no distinction in wages between male and female employees (note 3 for shops, note 3 for guards).
  • Where the existing wages are higher than the minimum rates fixed, the higher wages continue to be paid (note 4).

What is in the Gazette that we did not transcribe

The shops notification also has a list of special categories (chemist and druggist shops, hair dressing salons, photo studios, service shops, optical shops, block making, diagnostic laboratories, chit funds, boarding and lodges, furniture, miscellaneous workers) with their own rates; we did not transcribe it because the printed groupings were not checked against the page images. The Gazette also carries notifications for electronic industry, food processing, general engineering and fabrication, chemical industries, plastic industries, leather goods, hotels and restaurants, oil mills, automobile workshops, tailoring, distribution, bakeries, rice, flour and dhall mills, taxis and autorickshaws, carpentry and blacksmith industries, bricks and tiles, and paper products; we state nothing for them.

What we do not state

Puducherry is a Union Territory. The Gazette is from September 2024, so a later revision may exist, and the Puducherry Labour Department sets the dearness allowance every year. We did not read the Code on Wages, 2019 or any rule under it. Check the Puducherry Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for a shop assistant in Puducherry?

Rs 11,131 a month for 8 hours a day, before dearness allowance, in the Gazette of 20 September 2024. The allowance is added on top.

What is the basic minimum wage for a security guard in Puducherry?

Rs 14,550 a month before dearness allowance, in the Security Guards notification of 2 September 2024 printed in the same Gazette.

How is the dearness allowance worked out?

For shops: Rs 32.40 per month for each point the average Puducherry City index is above 426; for guards Rs 45.20 per point above 366, each calculated every 1 April.
