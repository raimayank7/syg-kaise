West Bengal minimum wages, 1 July to 31 December 2026

West Bengal updates its minimum wages every six months in a circular listing every scheduled employment. This page restates the circular of 22 June 2026, which we read in full.

What the circular says

The circular lists the minimum rates of wages for employees employed in the scheduled employments in West Bengal, effective 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2026. It says the rates have been updated through the fixation or revision notifications named against each scheduled employment, as well as, in cases of Zone B, through the previous-period circular No. 07/Stat/14/RW/24/2023/LCS/JLC dated 9 January 2026 read with Labour Department notification No. I/723462/2025/LABR-25099/31/2025-LWMW dated 15 December 2025. The circular names, against each employment, the notification that fixed its rates: those of Set A are dated 29 December 2016 and those of Set B 20 December 2019. The two sets differ by a few rupees.

The two zones

The circular defines Zone A as areas under Municipal Corporations, Municipalities, Notified Areas, Development Authorities, and Thermal Power Plant areas including township areas. Zone B is the rest of West Bengal.

Notes in the circular

  • To arrive at the daily rate, the monthly rate is divided by 26, rounded off to the nearest rupee; for the weekly rate, the daily rate is multiplied by 6 (note 3).
  • A normal working day consists of eight hours of actual work and not less than half an hour of recess, subject to 48 hours of actual work in a week (note 4).
  • One day in any period of seven days is the weekly rest day, and the minimum rates include the wages for it. Work on the rest day and work beyond normal hours is paid at double the ordinary rates (note 5).
  • Where the existing rates of any employee, by contract, agreement or otherwise, are higher than the rates in the circular, the higher rates are protected (note 6).
  • The rates apply also to employees employed by contractors (note 7), and the rates for disabled persons are the same as for workers of the appropriate category (note 8).
  • Men and women get the same rates for the same work or work of similar nature (note 9).
  • The minimum rates of wages and variable dearness allowance, if any, together constitute the minimum rates enforceable under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (note 10).

The jobs under each category

The circular names the jobs that fall in each category of each employment, for example a peon, cleaner or security guard among the unskilled, and a nurse or X-ray technician among the skilled in hospitals. We have not copied those lists; read the circular for the category of a particular job.

What we do not state

We did not read the notifications named in the circular, the Code on Wages, 2019 as applied in West Bengal, or the circular of 9 January 2026. Rates are updated every six months, so the next circular will replace this one. Check the West Bengal Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Kolkata?

Kolkata is under a Municipal Corporation, so Zone A: Rs 10,558 a month and Rs 406 a day in most employments (Rs 10,556 in the five employments with Set B), from 1 July 2026 in the circular of 22 June 2026.

How is the daily rate worked out?

Note 3 says the monthly rate is divided by 26 and rounded to the nearest rupee.

Which employments does the circular cover?

Thirty-one scheduled employments, listed on this page. An employment not in the list is not covered by this circular.
