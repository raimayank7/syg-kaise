Uttar Pradesh minimum wages, 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027

Uttar Pradesh sets a basic minimum wage and adds a variable dearness allowance every six months. This page restates the Labour Department’s memorandum of 21 October 2026, which we read in Hindi.

Source

Read on 14 November 2026: the Uttar Pradesh Government Labour Department (Shram Anubhag-02) office memorandum (karyalaya-gyap) No. I/1498869/2026-2041256 dated Lucknow, 21 October 2026, which is in Hindi; we read it from the page images, and the copy we used was hosted by a compliance firm. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

How the memorandum builds the rate

The memorandum says the minimum wage rates for 74 scheduled employments were fixed by notification no. 374/36-2-2026-2041256 dated 17 April 2026, with the dearness neutralised on the All India Consumer Price Index (base 2001=100) up to an average of 400 points, taking the average of July to December 2023. Above 400 points, the variable dearness allowance is paid each April and October on the average index of the previous July to December and of January to June of the year concerned, with 100 per cent neutralisation of the rise.

For 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 the memorandum uses the average index of 431 for January to June 2026. Its worked example is (431 - 400) / 400 x 12,885 (the basic wage) = 998.59. Each row above adds that allowance to the basic wage in force from 1 April 2026 to give the monthly wage, and divides the monthly wage by 26 for the daily wage.

Three district categories

The memorandum prints three tables: the first for Gautam Buddh Nagar and Ghaziabad, the second for the districts of Uttar Pradesh that have a municipal corporation, and the third for the districts other than those of the first and second categories. We state no list of the districts of the second category, because the memorandum does not print one.

Notes in the memorandum, as we read them

  • The cost of the necessary items and the cash value of concessions will be looked at once a year, before 1 April and 1 October, using the average consumer price index published by the Labour Bureau of the Government of India for industrial workers (note 1).
  • The daily rate shall not be less than one twenty-sixth of the minimum basic wage plus the variable dearness allowance (note 2).
  • A worker whose hours, including the rest interval, are under 6 hours a day or 36 hours a week is treated as part-time, and the hourly wage shall not be less than one eighth of the corresponding daily rate (note 3).
  • These rates shall not work against the interest of any employee: if the present wage is higher, it continues (note 4).
  • Where work is done at piece rates, the guaranteed rate is the time rate fixed here (note 5).
  • A worker engaged through a contractor is treated as employed directly by the employer for these purposes and is entitled to the same wages and dearness allowance (note 6).
  • The minimum time rate of wages for adolescents shall not be less than the time rate applicable to an adult employee (note 7).
  • The minimum time rates fixed include the remuneration for the rest day allowed under section 13 of the Code on Wages, 2019 (note 8).

What we do not state

We did not read the notification of 17 April 2026 that fixed the basic wages for the 74 scheduled employments, so we show only the three skill categories the memorandum prints, and nothing for any particular employment. We did not read the Code on Wages, 2019 or the Uttar Pradesh rules. Rates are revised every six months, so a memorandum for 1 April 2027 will replace this one. Check the Uttar Pradesh Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Ghaziabad?

Rs 13,883.59 a month and Rs 533.98 a day from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027, in the memorandum of 21 October 2026 (Category I).

How is the daily wage worked out?

Note 2 says the daily rate shall not be less than one twenty-sixth of the monthly minimum wage including the variable dearness allowance.

Does this apply to every employment?

The memorandum speaks of the 74 scheduled employments fixed by the notification of 17 April 2026, which we did not read, so we cannot say which employments are covered.
