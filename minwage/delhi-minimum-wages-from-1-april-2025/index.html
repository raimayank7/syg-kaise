Delhi minimum wages from 1 April 2025

Delhi revises the dearness allowance on its minimum wages every six months. This page restates the order of 15 April 2025, the latest one on the Labour Department’s minimum wage page when we looked.

Read on 14 November 2026: the order of the Office of the Commissioner (Labour), Government of NCT of Delhi, F. No. (142)/02/MW/VII/Part file/211-241 dated 15 April 2025, signed by the Joint Labour Commissioner (HQ), downloaded from the Labour Department’s own site (labour.delhi.gov.in) and read from the scan. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the order says

The order recalls that the Government of the National Capital Territory of Delhi last revised the minimum rates of wages in scheduled employments under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 by notification no. 12(142)/02/MW/VII/3636 dated 22 October 2019, and that this notification stipulated that dearness allowance is payable on the basis of six-monthly average index numbers, January to June and July to December, on 1 April and 1 October respectively.

After adjustment for the average All India Consumer Price Index number of July to December 2024, which the order gives as 413.42, an increase of 11.32 points, the order declares dearness allowances payable for all categories with effect from 1 April 2025.

How the rates are built

For unskilled, semi-skilled and skilled workers in all scheduled employments, the order starts from the rate as on 1 October 2024 and adds the dearness allowance for the month: unskilled Rs 18,066 + Rs 390 = Rs 18,456; semi-skilled Rs 19,929 + Rs 442 = Rs 20,371; skilled Rs 21,917 + Rs 494 = Rs 22,411, a day rate of Rs 710, Rs 784 and Rs 862 respectively.

For clerical and supervisory staff it prints: non matriculates Rs 19,929 + Rs 442 = Rs 20,371; matriculates but not graduates Rs 21,917 + Rs 494 = Rs 22,411; graduates and above Rs 23,836 + Rs 520 = Rs 24,356, with day rates of Rs 784, Rs 862 and Rs 937.

The order carries a tampering warning

A note at the foot of the order says cases of tampering with the order have come to the department’s notice and that employers and workers may confirm from the Labour Department website, labour.delhi.gov.in. We downloaded our copy from that site.

What may have changed since

Under the 2019 notification the dearness allowance is revised every 1 April and 1 October, so a newer order for 1 October 2025 or 1 April 2026 may exist. The Labour Department’s minimum wage page, as we fetched it on 14 November 2026, listed this April 2025 order as the newest download. We cannot tell whether a later order is published somewhere else, and secondary websites quote conflicting figures, so we do not use them. Check labour.delhi.gov.in before you pay or claim wages.

What we do not state

We did not read the 2019 notification, the Code on Wages, 2019 or any Delhi rules under it, and the order prints no hourly rate. The order is under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 as it says, and we have not checked how the Code on Wages affects it.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Delhi?

Rs 18,456 a month and Rs 710 a day from 1 April 2025, in the order of 15 April 2025. A later order may exist; check the Labour Department site.

How often does Delhi revise it?

The order says dearness allowance is payable on 1 April and 1 October on the basis of six-monthly average index numbers.
