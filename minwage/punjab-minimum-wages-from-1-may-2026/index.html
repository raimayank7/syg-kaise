Punjab minimum wages from 1 May 2026

Punjab fixed new basic minimum wages for all employments in the State from 1 May 2026. This page restates the notification, which we read in full.

Source

We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the notification fixes

The notification says the Governor of Punjab, after considering the report of the Advisory Board and after consultation with the Punjab Minimum Wages Advisory Board, fixes or revises the basic minimum rates of wages in respect of all employments in the State of Punjab, in force from 1 May 2026. The tables above are the rates it prints, in rupees.

The four staff categories

The notification defines Staff Category A as a person with a post graduation degree, MBA or other professional degree or Company Secretary or equivalent. Category B is a graduate in any discipline, or an undergraduate with a diploma or certificate in stenography, computer applications or accountancy, or supervisory staff who take independent decisions. Category C is a person above matriculate but not a graduate or undergraduate, with a certificate as steno typist, computer application, data entry operator or accountancy, and it includes timekeeper, salesman, assistant and storekeeper. Category D is any Class-IV employee, casual, through a contractor or on contract.

Agriculture

For attached labour in agriculture (unskilled workers) the notification prints a consolidated yearly rate with meals or food grains equivalent of Rs 90,973.28 from 1 May 2026. For other agriculture labour it prints a daily rate of Rs 486.588 with meals and Rs 540.983 without meals. It says the rates for brick kilns will be notified separately.

Notes in the notification

  • The rates are basic rates which the employer is not permitted to split into allowances. There shall be 100 per cent neutralisation of the rise or fall of the consumer price index on a pro rata basis, as prescribed in the Code on Wages, 2019 and its rules (note 1).
  • The daily rate is the monthly rate divided by 26. If a day’s wage is deducted for absence, the daily wage is the monthly wage divided by 30 (note 7). The daily rate includes the weekly or substituted rest day (note 16).
  • No difference between the wages of men, women, transgender and disabled employees (note 4).
  • Where workers are employed through a contractor or service provider, the occupier or principal employer is personally responsible for payment of the minimum wages (note 5), and the same applies to shops and commercial establishments (note 11).
  • A trainee can be paid 75 per cent of the wage of the category, or the unskilled minimum wage, whichever is more, for six months or the period of training, whichever is less; no employer may engage an unskilled employee as a trainee (note 8).
  • In hotels, restaurants, tea stalls, halwais and similar places, and for domestic workers, a worker given two meals, two times tea and lodging may be paid 30 per cent less; with two meals and two times tea without lodging, 10 per cent less (note 13).
  • The rates are for normal working days of 8 hours for an adult (note 15).
  • The notification says it is amended from time to time by any notification of the Central or State Government (note 18).

What we do not state

We did not read the Code on Wages, 2019 or its rules, the earlier Punjab notifications, or any later amendment, and the notification itself says rates are revised from time to time. Check the Punjab Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Punjab?

Rs 13,486 a month, Rs 518.69 a day and Rs 64.83 an hour in the notification of 1 May 2026.

How is the daily rate worked out?

Note 7 says the monthly rate is divided by 26 for the daily rate, and by 30 if a day’s wage is deducted for absence.

Can an employer split it into basic and allowances?

Note 1 says the rates are basic rates which are not permitted to be segregated into allowances.
