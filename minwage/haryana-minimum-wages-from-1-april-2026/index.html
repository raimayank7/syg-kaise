Haryana minimum wages from 1 April 2026

Haryana fixed new basic minimum wages for all employments from 1 April 2026. This page restates the Gazette notification, which we read in full.

Source

Read on 14 November 2026: the Haryana Government Gazette (Extraordinary) No. 51-2026/Ext. of 9 April 2026, Labour Department notification No. 2/25/26-2 Lab., issued under sub-section (3) of section 8 of the Code on Wages, 2019. We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

What the notification fixes

The notification says the Governor of Haryana, after considering the report of the Committee and Sub-Committee constituted under section 8 of the Code on Wages, 2019 by notification of 4 February 2026, fixes or revises the basic minimum rates of wages for all the employments in the State, with effect from 1 April 2026. It says these were previously fixed by Haryana Labour Department notification 3/42/83-3 Lab. dated 21 October 2015.

Scope

The rates apply in all establishments defined under section 2(m), factories defined under section 2(n), and include employees as defined in section 2(k), workers under section 2(z) and contract labour under section 2(g) of the Code on Wages, 2019. The rates for brick kilns will be notified separately.

Notes in the notification

  • The rates are basic rates which are not permitted to be segregated into allowances. There shall be 100 per cent neutralisation of the rise or fall of the consumer price index on a pro rata basis, as prescribed in the Code on Wages Rules, 2026 (note 1).
  • The rates shall not be affected by linkage, so wages shall not fall below those now fixed (note 2).
  • Apprentices are governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961 (note 3).
  • No difference between the wages of men and women workers (note 4).
  • Where workers are engaged through a contractor or service provider, the occupier or principal employer is personally responsible for payment (note 5).
  • No worker shall be paid less than the minimum wage for a similar category with the same skills and experience (note 6).
  • The daily wage is the monthly wage divided by 26 days; deductions are calculated as monthly wages divided by 30 days or as the appropriate government prescribes (note 7).
  • A trainee shall be paid 75 per cent of the wages of the category but not less than the unskilled minimum wage; the period of training shall not be more than one year (note 8).

What we do not state

The notification prints no hourly rate and no staff categories, so we show none. We did not read the Code on Wages, 2019, the Haryana rules, or any later notification, and rates are revised from time to time. Check the Haryana Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Haryana?

Rs 15,220.71 a month and Rs 585.41 a day, in the Gazette notification of 9 April 2026.

How long can training last?

Note 8 says the period of training shall not be more than one year, and a trainee gets at least the unskilled rate.
