Jharkhand minimum wages from 1 October 2026

Jharkhand adds a variable dearness allowance to a basic minimum wage fixed in March 2024. This page restates the notification of 15 October 2026, which we read in Hindi and in its English translation.

We read the notification in full. It is a State notification: it applies in that State only.

How the notification builds the rate

The notification says that, in exercise of the powers under section 6 of the Code on Wages, 2019, the Governor of Jharkhand declares the variable dearness allowance on the basis of the average consumer price index for the different categories of employees in all employments, for the districts and areas classified in its Schedule 1. The basic minimum rates were fixed or revised after the index average of January to June 2023 (386 points) and the meeting of the Jharkhand Minimum Wage Advisory Board, and they have been in force from 11 March 2024.

For the first half of 2026 the notification takes the All India Consumer Price Index (2016 = 100) for January to June 2026: 148.6, 148.5, 149.1, 149.9, 150.8 and 151.9, a total of 898.8 and an average of 149.8. It converts this to the old series (2001 = 100) as 149.8 x 2.88 = 431.42, or 431, an increase of 45 points over 386, which it states as an increase of 11.65 per cent. The variable dearness allowance on that average is payable from 1 October 2026.

The three zones

Schedule 1 classifies the areas into three. Zone A is the area of the Ranchi, Chas, Dhanbad, Deoghar and Hazaribagh municipal corporations and the Jamshedpur notified area, the Mango municipal corporation, the Jugsalai municipal council area and the Adityapur municipal corporation area. Zone B is the urban local body area (municipal corporations, municipal councils and nagar panchayats) of all other districts, other than Zone A. Zone C is the other area of the State.

Notes in the notification

  • The basic rates of minimum wage were reviewed on the index of 386 points, the average of January to June 2023 and the advisory board’s consensus (note a).
  • The average index for January to June 2026 is 431, on which the dearness allowance is payable (note b).
  • On the advisory board’s consensus, the variable dearness allowance has been rounded off to a whole number (note c).
  • The rates come under sub-section (1) of section 6 of the Code on Wages, 2019 (note d).
  • Monthly rates are converted to daily rates by dividing by 26, and daily rates to monthly by multiplying by 26 (note e).
  • The rates on which the variable dearness allowance is effective are applicable in all establishments (paragraph 5 of the notification).
  • The wage rates will rise or fall with the index and the change is to be treated as variable dearness allowance; the dearness allowance rates are not to fall even if the index goes below the point on which they were fixed (paragraph 3).

What we do not state

The notification gives the same rates for every employment; we state no rate for a particular trade and no overtime rule. We did not read the notification of 11 March 2024 that fixed the basic rates, the Code on Wages, 2019 or the Jharkhand rules. The dearness allowance is revised every six months, so a notification for 1 April 2027 will replace this one. Check the Jharkhand Labour Department before you pay or claim wages.

Common questions

What is the minimum wage for an unskilled worker in Ranchi?

Ranchi is in Zone A: Rs 523 a day (468 + 55) and Rs 13,586 a month (12,168 + 1,418) from 1 October 2026 in the notification of 15 October 2026.

How is the daily rate related to the monthly one?

Note (e) says the monthly rate is divided by 26 to get the daily rate, and the daily rate is multiplied by 26 to get the monthly rate.

From when do these rates apply?

The Hindi notification says it takes effect from 1 October 2026; the basic rates in it have applied since 11 March 2024.
