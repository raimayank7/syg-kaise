Know your rights: FIR, arrest and bail, explained

Plain guides cited to the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 and Article 22 of the Constitution, plus a sample application to fill in, copy and print. Free, no login.

Guides

  • How to file an FIR: section 173 of the BNSS
  • Your rights when the police arrest you: BNSS sections 35, 36, 38, 47, 48
  • After an arrest: 24 hours, the Magistrate and custody
  • Bail: sections 478, 479 and 480 of the BNSS

Sample letter

  • Application to the Superintendent of Police when the police refuse to record an FIR

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

What we leave out on purpose

We do not state any offence or punishment, because we did not read the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, and we do not predict what a court will do. What we do state is tied to a section we read on 13 November 2026.

Do you store what I type?

No. The sample is filled inside your browser and nothing is sent to us.
