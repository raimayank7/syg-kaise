Affidavit mistakes to avoid

Most rejected affidavits fail for ordinary reasons. Run through this list before you swear.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 13 November 2026: the Oaths Act, 1969 (India Code copy, "As on the 6th Oct, 2025"); the Notaries Act, 1952 (India Code copy); the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, Order XIX (India Code copy).

The checklist

  • Name, father's or husband's name, age and address match your identity documents exactly.
  • Every paragraph states a fact you know yourself (CPC Order XIX Rule 3(1) is the court rule; it is also the safe habit elsewhere).
  • No hearsay, no arguments, no pasted documents (Rule 3(2) can make you pay costs in a court case).
  • No blank spaces and no overwriting.
  • The verification matches the Form No. 4 idea: you swear or affirm that the name and signature are yours and the contents are true.
  • You sign only in front of the person taking the oath.
  • The notary signs and puts the official seal (Notaries Act s.8(2)).
  • You read the final copy at the counter, not at home later.

Mistakes that cost time

Copying a draft from the internet that names another person or another purpose; leaving the date or place empty; signing in advance; bringing a photocopy of an ID when the original is required; using a different spelling of your name from your other documents.

If a fact changes

If something in an attested affidavit later turns out wrong, do not alter the paper. Ask the office or your lawyer about a corrective affidavit. Section 7 of the Oaths Act, which covers irregularities in the oath, does not cure a false fact.

When to ask a lawyer

For court cases, property transfers, inheritance, and anything where someone may dispute your statement. An affidavit is a statement under oath, so false statements can have legal consequences, which this site does not describe.

Common questions

Can I correct an affidavit by hand after attestation?

Do not. Ask the notary and the office how to correct it, usually by making a fresh affidavit.
