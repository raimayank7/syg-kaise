Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

What we leave out on purpose

Stamp-paper values, notary fees, court fees and rules that differ from state to state are not stated, because they change and we could not verify them for every state. Where they matter, a page says to ask the notary, the office or a lawyer. What we do state is tied to a section of an Act we read in full on 13 November 2026.
