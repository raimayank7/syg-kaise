Refund policy generator

A clear refund policy reduces disputes and chargebacks, and it makes first-time buyers more willing to order. Choose what you sell, set your days and copy the text.

A generated policy is a starting point, not legal advice. It is a general draft written from your answers. Read it, check it against what your site really does, and ask a lawyer if your case is not simple.

What a good refund policy says

Buyers look for four answers: can I return it, within how many days, who pays the shipping, and how soon do I get my money. The generator covers all four, in separate paragraphs for physical goods, digital downloads and services, so you can tick the ones you sell.

Be generous only where you can afford it. A seven-day window with free returns on faulty items is a fair, common arrangement for small sellers. Digital downloads are normally not refunded after access, except when the file is faulty.

Check the consumer law of your country

A refund policy cannot take away the rights a buyer has under the law, and those rights differ by country and sometimes by state. We have not read the consumer laws for this page, so we state none. The generated text includes a line saying your legal rights are not affected. Before you finalise your days, read the rules for your country on the government's consumer protection website, or ask a lawyer.

Where to show it

  • A link in the footer of every page.
  • A link on the product page and at checkout, before payment.
  • A copy in order confirmation emails.
  • The same promise on any marketplace listing.

Common questions

Can I say "no refunds" at all?

You can limit refunds, but you cannot take away a buyer's legal rights for faulty or misdescribed goods. "All sales final" does not override consumer law.

How long should my return window be?

Seven to thirty days is usual for small shops. Pick a number you can keep and write it exactly.

Who pays return shipping?

Usually you, when the item is faulty or wrong; the buyer, when they simply changed their mind. The form lets you choose.
