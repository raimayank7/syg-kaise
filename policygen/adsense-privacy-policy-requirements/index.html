AdSense and the privacy policy

If you show AdSense ads, your privacy policy has a job beyond the law: Google requires certain statements in it. This checklist is built from Google's own pages.

Sources

Sources read on 13 November 2026: Google AdSense Help, "Required content" (support.google.com/adsense/answer/1348695) and Google's EU user consent policy (google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy). Google changes these pages; read the current versions before you apply.

What your policy must include

On its "Required content" page, under "My privacy policy", Google lists what your privacy policy should include. In our words:

  • That third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads on your site.
  • That visitors may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Where other vendors' cookies may also serve ads, that should be disclosed as well.
  • Notify visitors of the third-party vendors and ad networks used, with links to them and an opt-out notice.

Visitors in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

Google's EU user consent policy applies to end users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland where your agreement with Google or the Google product incorporates it. It requires you to obtain legally valid consent to the use of cookies or other local storage where the law requires it, and to the collection, sharing and use of personal data for ad personalisation. You must keep records of consent, give end users clear instructions to revoke it, and identify each party that may collect or use their data with easy access to information about its use. Google may limit or suspend your use of its products if you do not comply.

How to do it with our generators

Tick "Advertising" and "Google AdSense" in the privacy policy generator, and make a cookie policy with the advertising row ticked. Read the result against the list above and add the links to vendors and the opt-out pages yourself.

Other requirements

Google's other policies on content quality, required pages and ad placement are separate. Read the AdSense Program policies and Required content pages before applying. Nothing here promises approval.

Common questions

Where should the policy link be?

Make the policy reachable from every page of your site, for example in the footer. Read the Required content page for what Google expects.

Is a generated policy enough for AdSense?

It can include the statements listed above. Approval depends on Google's review of your whole site.
