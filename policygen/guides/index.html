How to use these guides

Read the guide for the law that covers your visitors, then make your policy with the matching generator and check it against the checklist in the guide. The guides describe ideas rather than quote section numbers, because numbers and thresholds change. When a guide says to check the official text, it names the place to look.

General starting point, not legal advice. Facts about laws were last read on 13 November 2026; check the official text for your case.
