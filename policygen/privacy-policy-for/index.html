How these presets work

Each page opens the same generator with the ticks a typical site of that kind needs: a blog ticks comments, newsletter and ads; an online store ticks accounts, orders and a payment provider; a portfolio ticks only a contact form. You then change anything that is not true for you. The preset is a starting point, never a promise that your site collects exactly this, so read the final text and compare it with what your site does.

If your site is a mix, such as a blog that also sells a course, start with the closest page and tick what is missing. If you are not sure which services your site loads, test it with a free cookie scanner or open the browser developer tools, look at the Network tab and see which addresses load besides your own.
