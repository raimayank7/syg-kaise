Why these pages matter

A privacy policy is expected by visitors, required by advertising and payment services, by the app stores, and in many places by law. Terms of use and a disclaimer protect you from the most common disputes. A refund policy reassures buyers. This site writes each of them in plain words from your answers.

Written for India first

Most generators assume an American site. This one starts from India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and adds the European GDPR and US state laws when your visitors are there. It also reminds you of AdSense and app store needs, and explains how to publish the pages on Blogger, WordPress and Wix. See the guides.

General starting point, not legal advice. Facts about laws were last read on 13 November 2026; check the official text for your case.
