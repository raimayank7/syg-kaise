RTI first appeal: what section 19(1) says

If you get no reply, or you are not satisfied, the first appeal goes to a senior officer in the same public authority. This page follows section 19(1), (5) and (6).

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 13 November 2026: the Right to Information Act, 2005 (Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 21 June 2005, the copy kept at rti.gov.in, read through an Internet Archive snapshot of 2 April 2025) and the Right to Information Rules, 2012 (G.S.R. 603(E), 31 July 2012, the copy on cic.gov.in). We read the Act as originally published and the Rules of 2012. Later amendments of the Act (other than section 8(1)(j), see our page on personal information) and any amending rules were not read, so check the current text on the India Code or the Department of Personnel and Training site before you rely on a figure.

Who can appeal and where: section 19(1)

Any person who does not receive a decision within the time in section 7(1), or is aggrieved by a decision of the Public Information Officer, may within thirty days from the expiry of that period or from receipt of the decision prefer an appeal to an officer senior in rank to the Public Information Officer in the same public authority. Rule 2(c) of the Central Rules calls that officer the First Appellate Authority.

A late appeal

The proviso to section 19(1) lets the officer admit an appeal after the thirty days if satisfied that the appellant was prevented by sufficient cause from filing in time. Say why you are late in the appeal.

Who must prove what: section 19(5)

In any appeal proceedings, the onus to prove that a denial of a request was justified lies on the Public Information Officer who denied it.

How long the appeal takes: section 19(6)

An appeal under section 19(1) or (2) shall be disposed of within thirty days of receipt, or within such extended period not exceeding a total of forty-five days from the date of filing, for reasons recorded in writing.

What to attach

The Act does not set a form or fee for the first appeal, and we found none in the Central Rules we read. Attach a copy of your original application, proof of its date, the reply or a note that none came, and the reasons you disagree. Our sample first-appeal letter follows these points.

If the first appeal fails

A second appeal lies to the Central or State Information Commission within ninety days; see our second-appeal page.

Common questions

Do I pay a fee for the first appeal?

We found no appeal fee in the Act or the Central Rules we read. A state may have its own rules; check them.

Can the same officer decide my first appeal?

Section 19(1) sends it to an officer senior in rank to the Public Information Officer.
