RTI second appeal format (section 19(3))

A letter of appeal to the Information Commission after the first appeal failed or was not decided. The Commission may have its own format; check its notices.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

To, The Registrar, The [Central / State] Information Commission [Address of the Commission] Date: [Date] Subject: Second appeal under section 19(3) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 Appellant: [Your full name and address] Respondents: (1) The Public Information Officer, [Authority]; (2) The First Appellate Authority, [Authority] 1. I applied to the Public Information Officer on [Date of application] for [One line on the information sought]. 2. [Choose one: I received no decision. / I received the decision dated [Date] refusing or giving [One line].] 3. I filed a first appeal on [Date of first appeal] before the First Appellate Authority. [Choose one: It was decided on [Date of order] by an order that [One line]. / No final order has been made and forty-five days have passed from the date of filing, so under rule 10(3) of the Right to Information Rules, 2012 the remedies are deemed availed of.] 4. This appeal is within ninety days of the date the decision should have been made or was received, as section 19(3) requires. [If late: I ask you to admit it for the following sufficient cause: [Reason].] 5. I pray that the Commission direct the Public Information Officer to provide the information and take any steps it considers necessary under section 19(8). Documents enclosed (rule 8): 1. copy of my application; 2. copy of the reply received, if any; 3. copy of my first appeal; 4. copy of the order of the First Appellate Authority, if any; 5. [Other documents relied on]; 6. index of the documents. Appellant’s signature: ______________________ [Your full name] Phone or e-mail: [Phone or e-mail]

What this sample is for

A letter of appeal to the Information Commission after the first appeal failed or was not decided. The Commission may have its own format; check its notices.

Which parts of the Act and Rules it follows

  • Section 19(3): second appeal within ninety days of the date the decision should have been made or was received, with the Central or State Information Commission; late appeals may be admitted for sufficient cause.
  • Rule 8 of the Central Rules: the listed documents, authenticated and verified by the appellant, with an index.
  • Rule 10(2) and (3): the Commission considers an appeal only after the remedies under the Act are used; deemed if the First Appellate Authority has made a final order or forty-five days have passed from the date of the first appeal.
  • Section 19(7) and (8): the Commission’s decision is binding and it can direct the steps listed.

Check before you send

  • Check the Commission’s website for its own format, address and any fee or online filing method.
  • Authenticate and verify each copy as rule 8 requires.
  • Keep the postal receipt or the portal acknowledgement.
  • This sample is not the Appendix format of the Rules, which we did not read.

Sources

Read on 13 November 2026: the Right to Information Act, 2005 (Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 21 June 2005, the copy kept at rti.gov.in, read through an Internet Archive snapshot of 2 April 2025), and the Right to Information Rules, 2012 (G.S.R. 603(E), 31 July 2012, the copy on cic.gov.in). We read the Act as originally published and the Rules of 2012. Later amendments of the Act (other than section 8(1)(j), see our page on personal information) and any amending rules were not read, so check the current text on the India Code or the Department of Personnel and Training site before you rely on a figure.
