RTI second appeal: Information Commission, section 19(3)

A second appeal goes to the Central Information Commission (or the State Information Commission). This page follows section 19(3), (7), (8) and rules 8 to 10 of the Central Rules.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 13 November 2026: the Right to Information Act, 2005 (Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 21 June 2005, the copy kept at rti.gov.in, read through an Internet Archive snapshot of 2 April 2025) and the Right to Information Rules, 2012 (G.S.R. 603(E), 31 July 2012, the copy on cic.gov.in). We read the Act as originally published and the Rules of 2012. Later amendments of the Act (other than section 8(1)(j), see our page on personal information) and any amending rules were not read, so check the current text on the India Code or the Department of Personnel and Training site before you rely on a figure.

Time limit: section 19(3)

A second appeal against the decision under section 19(1) lies within ninety days from the date the decision should have been made or was actually received, with the Central Information Commission or the State Information Commission. The proviso lets the Commission admit it later if satisfied that the appellant was prevented by sufficient cause.

What rule 8 asks you to file

Rule 8 says an appeal to the Commission is filed in the format given in the Appendix and is accompanied by documents duly authenticated and verified by the appellant, namely a copy of the application to the Public Information Officer; a copy of the reply received, if any; a copy of the appeal to the First Appellate Authority; a copy of the order received, if any, from that authority; copies of other documents relied upon and referred to in the appeal; and an index of the documents. We did not read the Appendix format itself.

Return, dismissal and the remedies rule

Rule 9 says an appeal may be returned for removing the deficiencies if it lacks the documents in rule 8. Rule 10(1) lets the Commission dismiss an appeal it is not satisfied is fit to proceed with, after giving the appellant a hearing and recording reasons, and says no appeal is dismissed only because it is not in the specified format if the rule 8 documents are attached. Rule 10(2) says the Commission shall not consider an appeal unless the appellant has availed of all remedies under the Act, and rule 10(3) says that is deemed so if the First Appellate Authority has made a final order, or if no final order has been made and forty-five days from the date the first appeal was filed have passed.

Hearing

Rule 12 says the appellant is informed of the hearing date at least seven clear days before it, and may be present in person, through a duly authorised representative, or through video conferencing where available.

What the Commission can order: section 19(8) and (7)

The Commission’s decision is binding (section 19(7)). Under section 19(8) it can require the public authority to take steps to secure compliance, including providing access in a particular form, appointing a Public Information Officer, publishing information, changing record-keeping practices and training officials. It can also require the public authority to compensate the complainant for any loss or other detriment suffered, impose the penalties the Act provides, or reject the application.

Complaints, not only appeals: section 18

Section 18(1) also lets a person complain to the Commission, for example if no officer was appointed, if access was refused, if no response came within the time limit, if the fee demanded seems unreasonable, or if the information given seems incomplete, misleading or false.

Common questions

Is there a fee for the second appeal?

We found none in the Central Rules we read (rules 8 to 10). Check the Commission’s own notices.

Do I have to file a first appeal before the second?

Rule 10(2) says the Commission shall not consider an appeal unless you have availed of all the remedies under the Act, with the deemed position in rule 10(3).
