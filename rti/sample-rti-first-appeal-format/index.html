RTI first appeal format (section 19(1))

A letter to the officer senior to the Public Information Officer when no reply came in time or the reply is unsatisfactory.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

To, The First Appellate Authority, [Name of the public authority] [Address of the office] Date: [Date] Subject: First appeal under section 19(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 Sir/Madam, 1. I applied to the Public Information Officer, [Name or designation of the officer], on [Date of application] for the information described in the copy of my application enclosed. The office received it on [Date received]. 2. [Choose one: I received no decision within thirty days, which section 7(2) treats as a refusal. / I received a reply dated [Date of reply] that [State in one line what the officer said or refused].] 3. I am aggrieved because [State briefly why the reply is incomplete or the refusal is not justified, naming the section of the Act the officer relied on, if any]. 4. Under section 19(5) of the Act the onus to prove that a denial was justified is on the officer who denied the request. I request that you direct the Public Information Officer to provide the information, free of charge if the time limit was missed (section 7(6)), and decide this appeal within the time in section 19(6). [If more than thirty days have passed since the reply or the end of the period: My appeal is late because [State the reason]. I ask you to admit it, as the proviso to section 19(1) permits.] Name: [Your full name] Address: [Your full address] Phone or e-mail (optional): [Phone or e-mail] Signature: ______________________ Enclosures: copy of the application and fee proof; copy of the reply, if any; any other papers [List them]

What this sample is for

A letter to the officer senior to the Public Information Officer when no reply came in time or the reply is unsatisfactory.

Which parts of the Act and Rules it follows

  • Section 19(1): appeal within thirty days of the end of the period in section 7(1), or of receipt of the decision, to an officer senior in rank to the Public Information Officer; the proviso admits a late appeal for sufficient cause.
  • Section 19(5): the onus to prove that a denial was justified is on the officer who denied the request.
  • Section 19(6): disposal within thirty days, or an extended period not exceeding a total of forty-five days, for reasons recorded in writing.
  • Section 7(6): information free of charge where the time limits were not met.

Check before you send

  • Count thirty days from the end of the period or from the date you got the decision.
  • Attach the original application and proof of its date.
  • State facts and sections; avoid accusations.
  • Keep a copy and the date the office received your appeal.

Sources

We read the Act as originally published and the Rules of 2012. Later amendments of the Act (other than section 8(1)(j), see our page on personal information) and any amending rules were not read, so check the current text on the India Code or the Department of Personnel and Training site before you rely on a figure.
