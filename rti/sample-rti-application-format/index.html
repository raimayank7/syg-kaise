RTI application format (central public authority)

A short request for records from a Central Government office. For a state office, change the fee line to your state’s rule.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

To, The Central Public Information Officer, [Name of the public authority] [Address of the office] Date: [Date] Subject: Application under section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 Sir/Madam, I request the following information under the Right to Information Act, 2005: 1. [Describe the first record or information you want, with dates and file or reference numbers if you know them] 2. [Describe the second record or information, if any] 3. [Describe the third, if any] Form in which I want it: [Photocopies / inspection of records / electronic copy] Application fee: Rs 10 paid by [cash against receipt no. / demand draft or bankers cheque or Indian Postal Order no.] dated [Date], payable to the Accounts Officer of the public authority. [If you are below the poverty line, replace the fee line with: I am below the poverty line; a copy of my certificate issued by the appropriate Government is enclosed.] I will pay any further fee you ask for on receipt of the calculation, as section 7(3) provides. My name: [Your full name] My address: [Your full address with PIN code] Phone or e-mail (optional): [Phone or e-mail] Yours faithfully, ______________________ [Your full name] Enclosures: [List the fee proof and any other papers]

What this sample is for

A short request for records from a Central Government office. For a state office, change the fee line to your state’s rule.

Which parts of the Act and Rules it follows

  • Section 6(1): a request in writing or through electronic means, with the prescribed fee, specifying the particulars of the information sought; section 6(2): no reason needed.
  • Rule 3 of the Central Rules: fee of rupees ten, ordinarily not more than five hundred words excluding annexures.
  • Rule 4: rupees two for each page in A-3 or smaller size paper and the other charges, asked only after you receive the officer’s calculation (section 7(3)).
  • Rule 5: no fee from a person below the poverty line with the certificate.

Check before you send

  • Ask for records, files, copies or inspection, not for opinions or explanations.
  • Number each item so the officer can answer each.
  • Keep a copy and the proof of payment, and note the date the office received it.
  • If the matter concerns a person’s life or liberty, write that clearly: the proviso to section 7(1) sets forty-eight hours.

Sources

Read on 13 November 2026: the Right to Information Act, 2005 (Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 21 June 2005, the copy kept at rti.gov.in, read through an Internet Archive snapshot of 2 April 2025), and the Right to Information Rules, 2012 (G.S.R. 603(E), 31 July 2012, the copy on cic.gov.in). We read the Act as originally published and the Rules of 2012. Later amendments of the Act (other than section 8(1)(j), see our page on personal information) and any amending rules were not read, so check the current text on the India Code or the Department of Personnel and Training site before you rely on a figure.
