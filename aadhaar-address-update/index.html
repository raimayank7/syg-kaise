How to update your address in Aadhaar online

Change the address on your Aadhaar from home on the myAadhaar portal, with one proof of address and an OTP.

हिंदी में पढ़ें

Checked on 11 November 2026. Fees, forms and steps change. Confirm on the official website before you pay. This is not a government website, and we do not process any application.

What you need

  • Your Aadhaar number
  • The mobile number linked to your Aadhaar (the OTP comes on it)
  • A clear photo or scan of a proof of address in your own name, from the list of documents UIDAI accepts
  • A way to pay online (UPI, card or net banking)

Steps

Tick each step as you finish it. The ticks stay in your browser.

Fee

UIDAI's fee for a demographic update (name, address, date of birth, mobile or email) was reported as ₹75 when we checked. Only re-uploading your identity and address documents, without changing any detail, was reported free online until 14 June 2027. The portal shows the exact fee before you pay.

Good to know

  • Use only the official address, which ends in uidai.gov.in. Many look-alike websites charge extra or steal details.
  • Never share your Aadhaar OTP with anyone, including a person who says he is calling from UIDAI.
  • If no mobile number is linked to your Aadhaar, or you want to change the mobile number, photograph or fingerprints, you have to visit an Aadhaar enrolment centre. These cannot be done online.
  • If you have no address proof in your own name, look at the "Head of Family based address update" option on the portal.
  • A cyber cafe can help you fill the form, but watch the screen and enter the OTP yourself.

Official website

myAadhaar (UIDAI)

The link opens the official site in a new tab. Check that the address in your browser matches.
