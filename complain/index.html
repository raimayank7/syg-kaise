Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

What we leave out on purpose

The fee table (2020 Rules) and the money limits (a 2021 release) are stated with their dates. The filing portal, the helpline numbers and any other dated item carry their date on the page. What we do state is tied to a section of the Act we read on 13 November 2026. India only.

Do you store what I type?

No. The sample is filled inside your browser and nothing is sent to us.
