Consumer complaint letter to the seller or service provider

A clear written complaint to the seller, company or service provider, kept as proof of your dates before any case is filed.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

To, The [Manager / Customer care officer / Proprietor], [Name of the seller or company] [Address] Date: [Date] Subject: Complaint about [Product or service] — request for [Refund / Replacement / Repair / Removal of the deficiency] Sir/Madam, 1. I bought [Product or service] on [Date of purchase] from [Shop or website] for Rs [Amount paid], paid by [Cash / Card / UPI / Other]. Bill or order number: [Bill or order number]. 2. The problem is: [Describe what went wrong in two or three clear sentences]. 3. I first told you about it on [Date of first complaint] by [Phone / E-mail / In person]. [State what you were told or that there was no reply]. 4. I request that you [Refund Rs Amount / Replace the goods / Repair the goods / Remove the deficiency in the service] within [Number] days of this letter. 5. If the matter is not settled, I will be free to approach the Consumer Commission or any other forum open to me, and to claim the relief the law allows. Copies of the bill, order confirmation, photographs and earlier messages are enclosed. Yours faithfully, ______________________ [Your full name] [Your address] [Your phone or e-mail] Enclosures: [List the bill, photographs and messages]

What this sample is for

A clear written complaint to the seller, company or service provider, kept as proof of your dates before any case is filed.

Which parts of the Act it follows

  • Section 2(7): who is a consumer; section 2(11): what deficiency is.
  • Section 69(1): a Commission shall not admit a complaint filed after two years from the date the cause of action arose, so keep dates.
  • Section 39(1): the reliefs a Commission can order include replacement, refund with interest, compensation and costs.
  • Nothing in the Act we read requires this letter before a complaint; it records your dates and your request.

Check before you send

  • Send it by a method that gives proof (registered post, courier with receipt, or an e-mail you keep).
  • Keep a copy and the proof of sending.
  • State facts and dates only; do not threaten.
  • Check the Commission’s own rules for the documents it wants before you file.

Sources
