What a Consumer Commission can order: section 39

If the Commission is satisfied that your complaint is proved, section 39(1) lists what it can order. This page lists those reliefs as written.

When the section applies

Section 39(1) applies where the District Commission is satisfied that the goods complained against suffer from any of the defects specified in the complaint, or that the allegations about the services or any unfair trade practices, or the claims for compensation under product liability, are proved. It then issues an order directing the opposite party to do one or more of the following.

The reliefs in section 39(1)

  • (a) remove the defect pointed out by the appropriate laboratory from the goods;
  • (b) replace the goods with new goods of similar description, free from any defect;
  • (c) return to the complainant the price or charges paid, along with such interest as may be decided;
  • (d) pay compensation for any loss or injury suffered by the consumer due to the negligence of the opposite party, and the proviso says the Commission has power to grant punitive damages in such circumstances as it deems fit;
  • (e) pay compensation in a product liability action under Chapter VI;
  • (f) remove the defects in goods or deficiencies in the services;
  • (g) discontinue the unfair trade practice or restrictive trade practice and not repeat it;
  • (h), (i) and (j) not offer, to withdraw, and to cease manufacture of hazardous or unsafe goods and services;
  • (k) pay a sum where loss or injury has been suffered by a large number of consumers not conveniently identifiable, with a proviso that the minimum amount shall not be less than twenty-five per cent of the value of the defective goods sold or service provided;
  • (l) issue a corrective advertisement to neutralise the effect of a misleading advertisement, at the cost of the party responsible;
  • (m) provide for adequate costs to parties; and
  • (n) cease and desist from issuing any misleading advertisement.

What to ask for in your complaint

Ask only for what fits your facts: for a defective product, replacement or refund with interest and compensation for loss; for poor service, removal of the deficiency, refund and compensation. State the amounts you claim and how you worked them out.

Where some sums go

Section 39(2) says any amount obtained under section 39(1) shall be credited to a fund and utilised as prescribed. We did not read how the fund works.

Common questions

Can I get my money back with interest?

Section 39(1)(c) lets the Commission order the return of the price or charges paid along with such interest as may be decided.

Can the Commission order compensation for harassment?

Section 39(1)(d) speaks of compensation for loss or injury suffered due to the opposite party’s negligence, and allows punitive damages as the Commission deems fit. We do not predict amounts.
