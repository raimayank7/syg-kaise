National Consumer Helpline: numbers and channels (as archived)

Before a formal case, you can register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline. This page lists what its portal page showed in an archived copy.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Source and date

The Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, runs the National Consumer Helpline. Source: its portal consumerhelpline.gov.in as kept by the Internet Archive on 8 October 2024, read in a browser on 13 November 2026. The live page was not reached, so numbers and timings may have changed; confirm on the portal before you rely on them.

Ways to register a grievance, as the page listed them

  • Call 1800-11-4000 or 1915.
  • Timing shown: all days except national holidays, 08:00 AM to 08:00 PM.
  • SMS on 8800001915.
  • Register online (one-time consumer signup).
  • Through the NCH app, the UMANG app, or WhatsApp (the page offered a link to details for each).
  • Upload documents for a grievance already lodged, and track a grievance already lodged.

Other items on the same page

The page offers consumer rights text, FAQs on digital payments and cyber fraud (financial fraud, job fraud, matrimonial fraud, safe use of social media), a link to locate your Consumer Commission, links to the National, State and District Commissions, annual reports, an initiative to register grievances on misleading advertisements (GAMA), and a login for companies and regulators. For grievances related to airlines it points to airsewa.gov.in.

What it says a consumer is

“A Consumer is a person who purchases a product or avails a service for a consideration, either for his personal use or to earn his livelihood by means of self employment”, and the consideration may be paid, promised or partly paid and partly promised. It adds that the term includes a beneficiary of such goods or services when the use is made with the approval of the buyer. This matches section 2(7) of the Act that we read.

What the helpline is not

The helpline is a grievance route, not a Commission. It does not replace a complaint to a District Commission, which section 69 limits to two years from the cause of action. Keep to that limit while you try the helpline.

Common questions

Is the helpline number 1915 or 1800-11-4000?

The archived page of 8 October 2024 listed both. Confirm on consumerhelpline.gov.in.

Does calling the helpline stop the two-year limit?

We found nothing in section 69 that does; keep to the limit and ask a lawyer if you are close to it.
