After you get an FSSAI licence: returns, bills, changes and closing down

A licence comes with duties. This page lists the ones in FSSAI’s FAQs: returns, warranty on bills, changes, nominees and closing down.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the FSSAI FoSCoS “FAQs on FSSAI License and Registration” (page footer: last updated 21 June 2021), and the FoSCoS “Kind of Business” eligibility and fee table (a PDF whose own properties give the date 16 November 2023). Both documents are older than today; FSSAI can change fees, limits and rules, so check the FoSCoS portal (foscos.fssai.gov.in) or call its help desk before you apply.

Annual return

Licensed operators involved in manufacturing, including repacking and relabelling, and in importing food must submit an annual return in Form D1 by 31 May. Late filing attracts a late fee of Rs 100 a day until the return is submitted. A merchant exporter can file a quarterly report online.

Warranty on bills

Under clause 2.1.14 of the 2011 Licensing and Registration Regulations, every operator in business-to-business trade must give, separately or in the bill, cash memo or label, a warranty in Form E to the purchaser.

Changing the licence

A change that alters the information in the licence certificate (Form C) needs an application for modification with a fee of Rs 1,000 plus the difference in annual licence fee if the licence is upgraded. No fee is required for changes that are not on the certificate, such as the Form IX nominee or supporting documents. A change in production capacity is reported by a modification application; if it takes you from the State to the Central level you apply for a Central licence and surrender the State licence.

Products on the licence

Operators may manufacture or process only products that are endorsed on the licence. Making products without the endorsement is treated as conducting business without a licence. Fortified food, and organic food using the Jaivik Bharat logo, must be endorsed on the licence before the +F or organic logo may be used.

The nominee in Form IX

Every licensed operator informs the licensing authority in Form IX of the head or person in charge of the establishment, with certification that this person has been nominated for compliance. Every operator other than a proprietor needs to nominate someone.

Closing down, death, or a lost login

To surrender a licence, apply online from the FoSCoS account that made the application; it is treated as surrendered after approval. A licence can be transferred on the death of the owner by an online application with a death certificate and proof of the legal heir. If you lost your user ID and the recovery e-mail is not yours, create a new user ID and send an application signed by the authorised signatory, with a copy of the licence or Form A or B and an ID proof, to FSSAI headquarters (the FAQs name enforcement1@fssai.gov.in).

Inspection

Inspections are on the sanitary and hygienic requirements of Schedule 4 of the 2011 Regulations, and the checklists used by inspectors are based on Schedule 4 only, according to the FAQs.

Other permissions

There is no prerequisite for applying for an FSSAI licence, but you must have the permissions the District or local administration prescribes before you start the food business.

Common questions

Who files the Form D1 annual return?

Licensed manufacturers (including repackers and relabellers) and importers, by 31 May, according to FSSAI’s FAQs.

What does a change in the licence details cost?

Modification of a Form C detail carries a fee of Rs 1,000 plus the fee difference if upgraded; changes to the nominee or documents carry no fee, per the FAQs.
