FSSAI application returned, rejected, suspended or cancelled: what to do

Things can go wrong after you apply or after you hold a licence. This page lists what FSSAI’s FAQs say about each case and the time limits.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the FSSAI FoSCoS “FAQs on FSSAI License and Registration” (page footer: last updated 21 June 2021), and the FoSCoS “Kind of Business” eligibility and fee table (a PDF whose own properties give the date 16 November 2023). Both documents are older than today; FSSAI can change fees, limits and rules, so check the FoSCoS portal (foscos.fssai.gov.in) or call its help desk before you apply.

How long the authority takes

A registration certificate has to be issued within 7 days of the application; if the authority wants to inspect the premises, it must do so within 30 days of the application. A licence has to be issued within 60 days of a complete application, and that time includes scrutiny and inspection.

Application sent back with queries

Respond to the queries raised within 30 days. The application for editing or modification is on your dashboard. If you do not respond, the application is rejected.

Rejection

The licensing officer can reject an application if the required information is not provided within the time of 30 days or if the officer is convinced that the application is not tenable. Before refusing a licence the applicant is given an opportunity of being heard, and the reasons for refusal are recorded in writing.

Improvement notice

On receiving an improvement notice you must act on its suggestions; at least 14 days are granted for the compliance report. If you are aggrieved you may appeal under section 32 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.

Suspension and cancellation

The Designated Officer may suspend a licence if the operator is not complying with an improvement notice, and may cancel a suspended licence after a show cause notice and an opportunity to be heard. The operator must stop the food business at once on suspension or cancellation; carrying on is unlawful and attracts penal action. To revoke a suspension, take the remedial action stated in the improvement notice and inform the licensing authority, which may revoke with or without an inspection.

After cancellation

After 3 months from the date of cancellation under Regulation 2.1.8(3), the operator may apply afresh if all the observations in the improvement notice have been complied with.

Appeal

Under section 32(4) and 32(5) of the Act, a person aggrieved by the licensing or registering authority’s decision may appeal to the Commissioner of Food Safety, whose decision is final. The appeal period is 15 days from the day the notice of the decision was served; for an improvement notice it is 15 days or the period in the notice, whichever ends earlier. For a Central licence you can write to the Director of the FSSAI Regional Office.

Common questions

I did not answer the query in time.

The FAQs say the application is rejected if you do not respond within the 30 days.

How long do I have to appeal?

Fifteen days from service of the notice of the decision, to the Commissioner of Food Safety, under section 32 of the Act.

Can I go on trading while my licence is suspended?

No. The FAQs say you must stop food business activity at once.
