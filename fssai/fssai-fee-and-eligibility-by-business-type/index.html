FSSAI fee and eligibility by kind of business

Which level you need depends on your kind of business and its size. The table below repeats FSSAI’s own eligibility and fee table, with its date.

Also in the table

Departmental canteens at central government institutions pay Rs 2,000 for a Central licence above Rs 12 lakh turnover, and Rs 100 for a Central registration up to that. Caterers, restaurants and canteens at railway stations are licensed by the Railways at Rs 2,000 above Rs 12 lakh turnover, and Rs 100 for registration up to that. A mid-day meal caterer pays Rs 2,000 (State) up to Rs 20 crore turnover and Rs 7,500 (Central) above it; a mid-day meal canteen pays Rs 2,000 above Rs 12 lakh turnover and Rs 100 up to it.

How to use it

Find your kind of business, then compare your turnover or capacity with the limits. If your business has several activities at one premises, the FAQs say they can go on one licence. If your size passes a limit, the FAQs say to apply for the higher level and surrender the lower one.

Common questions

Is the fee yearly?

The table is headed “Fee per annum”, so the fees shown are per year. A licence can be applied for or renewed for 1 to 5 years at a time, according to the FAQs.

Is there a fee for registration?

The table shows Rs 100 for registration.

My business is not in the table.

Use the eligibility check on FoSCoS, or call FSSAI’s help desk; the FAQs give the toll-free number 1800112100.
