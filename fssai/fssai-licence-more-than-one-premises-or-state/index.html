FSSAI licence for more than one premises or State

If you run more than one shop, factory or godown, or work in more than one State, the licence rules change. This page follows FSSAI’s FAQs.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the FSSAI FoSCoS “FAQs on FSSAI License and Registration” (page footer: last updated 21 June 2021), and the FoSCoS “Kind of Business” eligibility and fee table (a PDF whose own properties give the date 16 November 2023). Both documents are older than today; FSSAI can change fees, limits and rules, so check the FoSCoS portal (foscos.fssai.gov.in) or call its help desk before you apply.

Each unit on its own

Separate licence or registration is obtained for each unit or premises, depending on that unit’s capacity or turnover, from the Central or State licensing authority concerned.

Two or more States

A food business operator whose premises are in two or more States must obtain a Central licence for the registered office or head office, in addition to the licence or registration for each unit. If any food business activity is carried on at the head office, it is also entered in the head office licence.

Importers and exporters

The FAQs say an FSSAI Central licence is mandatory for import and for export units, and that no NOC from FSSAI is required for export. An importer needs the Central licence at the address in the Import Export Code certificate from DGFT. The same IEC cannot be used in a second application unless the first application is deleted or the licence using it is surrendered.

Getting products made by someone else

An operator who gets products manufactured through third-party manufacturers must obtain a licence under the “Relabeller” kind of business. The eligibility is decided by the combined production capacity or the number of States where the third-party manufacturers are located.

Vending machines

Machines with annual turnover above Rs 12 lakh must be licensed. For machines with turnover up to Rs 12 lakh the parent entity obtains the licence, in line with FSSAI orders dated 8 July 2019 and 19 September 2019.

Railways, airports and seaports

Food premises in Railways are licensed by the Indian Railways Designated Officers. Airport and seaport premises are licensed by the Airport or Port Health Officers who are notified as Designated Officers, and otherwise by the Central Licensing Authority. Food business at an airport or seaport needs a Central licence, with a fee of Rs 7,500 a year according to the FAQs. All catering establishments, even under the Central Government, need a licence.

Common questions

I have shops in two States. How many licences?

One licence or registration for each shop, from the authority of its State, and a Central licence for the head office, according to the FAQs.

Do I need FSSAI permission to export food?

The FAQs say no NOC is needed, but the exporter needs an FSSAI licence.
