About Kaise Karein

Kaise Karein ("how to do it") is a free set of step-by-step guides to everyday government and money tasks in India, such as updating Aadhaar, getting a PAN, applying for a passport or reporting an online fraud. Every guide is available in English and in Hindi.

Where the content comes from

  • Every guide is our own writing. Nothing is copied from other websites.
  • The steps follow the official portals named in each guide: myAadhaar (UIDAI), the Income Tax e-filing portal, the Voters' Service Portal of the Election Commission of India, Passport Seva, Sarathi Parivahan, EPFO, IRCTC, DigiLocker, CEIR on Sanchar Saathi, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, the National Consumer Helpline and e-Jagriti, and RTI Online.
  • Fees, forms and rules were checked online on 11 November 2026. Where a figure was confirmed from news reports of an official notification and not from the official fee page itself, the guide says "as reported" (for example the passport fees revised from 1 July 2026, the Aadhaar update fee, the PAN card fee and the learner's licence fee). Figures we could not confirm are left out, and the guide tells you where the portal shows them.
  • Government procedures change often. Always confirm on the official website before you pay or submit. If you find something out of date, please tell us.

What this site is not

It is not a government website and is not connected with any department. We do not fill forms, accept documents or take payments, and nobody is authorised to do so in our name. The guides are general information, not legal, tax or financial advice.
