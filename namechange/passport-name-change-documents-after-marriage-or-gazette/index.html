Passport name change: which documents the booklet lists

A change of name on a passport is applied for as a re-issue. This page restates the cases and document numbers in the Passport Seva instruction booklet we read.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the Passport Seva "Instructions for filling of passport application form" booklet (ApplicationformInstructionBooklet-V3.0.pdf, 21 pages, downloaded from passportindia.gov.in on 14 November 2026); the UIDAI "Standard Operation Procedure (SOP) of Name & Gender Update Request under Exception Handling Process" (a 2021 circular, 6 pages, downloaded from uidai.gov.in on 14 November 2026); and the Gazette of India weekly issue No. 25 of 20 to 26 June 2026, Part IV (egazette.gov.in). We read the passport booklet, the UIDAI circular and one Gazette issue. We did not read the Department of Publications’ procedure or fee for publishing a notice, any State’s rules, or the rules for PAN, banks, voter ID or a driving licence, and the documents lists change, so use the passport portal’s Documents Advisor and UIDAI’s current list before you go.

How it is applied for

The booklet’s table “Change in Particulars” lists these cases under the re-issue of a passport. Each case names the documents to submit by number, listed in the booklet’s “Overall List of Documents”. The cases below are the ones about a name.

After marriage: a woman changing name or surname

The booklet lists documents 5, 14 or 56 for both normal and Tatkaal applications. Document 5 is the old passport in original with a self-attested photocopy of its first two and last two pages, including the ECR/non-ECR page and the page of observations, if any. Document 14 is an attested copy of the marriage certificate issued by the Registrar of Marriage. Document 56 is a joint photo declaration signed by both husband and wife as per Annexure J. So one of 14 or 56 goes with the old passport.

After divorce or separation

For divorcees or separated persons applying for a change of name or for deletion of the spouse’s name, the booklet lists document 5 and, if divorced, document 57: the divorce order or decree, if the name or surname change is based on the divorce.

Re-married applicants

The booklet lists documents 5 and 14 or 56, and, for the first spouse, document 25 or 33 as the case may be (we did not read what those two are).

A small change of spelling

For a minor change in name, meaning a spelling difference between the passport and other documents which phonetically does not result in a total change of name, the booklet lists document 5 only, for normal and Tatkaal applications.

A major change of name

For a major change in name (cases other than the minor one) the booklet lists documents 5, 13 and 55 and says it cannot be applied for under the Tatkaal scheme. Document 13 is clippings of two local newspapers or the Gazette notification of the concerned State Government, as the case may be. Document 55 is at least two public or school documents issued in the desired or applied changed name, to ascertain that the applicant has actually changed his or her name.

Government, public sector and statutory body employees

For a change of name in the case of such employees, the booklet lists documents 5, 15 and 16: the Gazette notification changing the name in the applicant’s department and a fresh ID certificate in the changed name.

What the booklet does not settle

The booklet has a fee list, which we do not state. Check the online Documents Advisor for your own case and the passport office’s notices, because these lists can change.

Common questions

Do I need a Gazette notice to add my husband’s surname?

For a woman changing name or surname due to marriage, the booklet lists documents 5, 14 or 56: the old passport and the marriage certificate or the joint photo declaration. It lists the newspaper or Gazette document (13) for a major change of name.

Can a change of name be done under Tatkaal?

The booklet lists the marriage and minor-change cases under both normal and Tatkaal, and says a major change in name cannot be applied for under the Tatkaal scheme.
