Aadhaar name update: the twice rule, exceptions and Gazette notification

UIDAI limits how many times a name can be updated and asks for a Gazette notification for a full name change. This page follows the UIDAI circular we read.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the Passport Seva "Instructions for filling of passport application form" booklet (ApplicationformInstructionBooklet-V3.0.pdf, 21 pages, downloaded from passportindia.gov.in on 14 November 2026); the UIDAI "Standard Operation Procedure (SOP) of Name & Gender Update Request under Exception Handling Process" (a 2021 circular, 6 pages, downloaded from uidai.gov.in on 14 November 2026); and the Gazette of India weekly issue No. 25 of 20 to 26 June 2026, Part IV (egazette.gov.in). We read the passport booklet, the UIDAI circular and one Gazette issue. We did not read the Department of Publications’ procedure or fee for publishing a notice, any State’s rules, or the rules for PAN, banks, voter ID or a driving licence, and the documents lists change, so use the passport portal’s Documents Advisor and UIDAI’s current list before you go.

The twice-in-a-lifetime rule

The circular says the existing practice of updating a name twice in a lifetime is revised as follows. For a minor name edit, such as a change in initials or surname, a name can be updated twice in a lifetime, with no change. For a full name change, where there was no explicit guideline, in case of a complete name change or a change in the first name of the resident, the Gazette notification of name change is to be uploaded as the Proof of Identity (PoI) document.

Corrections that do not count as a chance

The circular says these cases are not counted as an update request for the number of name-update chances: (i) correcting a typing mistake by an enrolment operator; (ii) correcting a transliteration error; and (iii) a name update consequent to an update of the resident’s regional language. The resident remains eligible for the balance chances, and a wrongly rejected request is reprocessed through the regional office.

After the two chances: exception handling

In case of exhaustion of name-update chances, the Regional Office may consider a request under exceptional handling only if conditions are satisfied. The circular names the common scenario of a marriage, divorce or adoption related change: the resident submits the marriage, divorce or adoption certificate, and the document is uploaded under PoI. It also names a request to append or edit initials, a change in the sequence of the existing name (for example Rajesh Prasad Sharma to Prasad Rajesh Sharma), and a complete change of name (for example Pankaj Kumar to Abhinay K Singh), where the Gazette notification of the name change is uploaded as the PoI document.

How to ask

For the exception cases, the circular says the resident enrols for the Aadhaar update at the nearest enrolment centre with the document and intimates UIDAI by mail; if the resident contacts UIDAI through 1947, the resident is advised to re-enrol and submit the enrolment ID (EID) by mail. The request is then verified at the Regional Office. A wrongly rejected request can be raised directly with the Regional Office or by letter, mail or by calling 1947.

What we do not state

The circular is from 2021 and says the list of documents would be added to the enrolment software after approvals. UIDAI may have changed its rules or fees since; check the current UIDAI page or call 1947 before you go. We state no fee.

Common questions

How many times can I change my name in Aadhaar?

The circular says a minor edit can be updated twice in a lifetime. After that, a marriage, divorce or adoption change can be considered under exception handling with the certificate.

Do I need a Gazette notification?

For a complete change of name or a change in the first name, the circular says the Gazette notification of name change is to be uploaded as the PoI document.
