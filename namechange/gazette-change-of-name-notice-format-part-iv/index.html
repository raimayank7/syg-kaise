Gazette change of name notice: the usual format (Part IV)

A Gazette notification is a published notice of your new name. This page shows the wording pattern in a real June 2026 issue and where other offices ask for it.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the Passport Seva "Instructions for filling of passport application form" booklet (ApplicationformInstructionBooklet-V3.0.pdf, 21 pages, downloaded from passportindia.gov.in on 14 November 2026); the UIDAI "Standard Operation Procedure (SOP) of Name & Gender Update Request under Exception Handling Process" (a 2021 circular, 6 pages, downloaded from uidai.gov.in on 14 November 2026); and the Gazette of India weekly issue No. 25 of 20 to 26 June 2026, Part IV (egazette.gov.in). We read the passport booklet, the UIDAI circular and one Gazette issue. We did not read the Department of Publications’ procedure or fee for publishing a notice, any State’s rules, or the rules for PAN, banks, voter ID or a driving licence, and the documents lists change, so use the passport portal’s Documents Advisor and UIDAI’s current list before you go.

Where the notices appear

The issue we read (No. 25, 20 to 26 June 2026) has Part IV, headed “Advertisements and Notices issued by Private Individuals and Private Bodies”. The heading “CHANGE OF NAME” introduces the notices from individuals.

The caution printed above them

Above the notices the Gazette prints, in capitals: no legal responsibility is accepted for the publication of advertisements or public notices in this part of the Gazette of India, and persons notifying them remain solely responsible for the legal consequences and for any other misrepresentation, by order of the Controller of Publication. Publishing a notice does not by itself make a document valid; it records your declaration.

The wording pattern

The notices we read follow one pattern: “I hitherto known as [old name with parentage and address] have changed my name and shall hereafter be known as [new name]. It is certified that I have complied with other legal requirements in this connection.” The notice is signed in the existing old name. Notices for minor children are made by a parent, naming the child. Our sample follows the pattern in our own wording.

When another office asks for it

The passport booklet lists the clippings of two local newspapers or the Gazette notification of the State Government for a major change of name (document 13), and the UIDAI circular asks for the Gazette notification of name change for a complete change of name or change in the first name. Offices differ; ask the office what it accepts.

What we did not read

The Department of Publications’ own instructions for submitting a notice, its fee and the time it takes, and the rules of any State for a State Gazette. Check egazette.gov.in and the Controller of Publications’ notices for them before you apply.

Common questions

Is a Gazette notice compulsory for a name change?

It is not required in every case we read. For a woman’s name or surname change after marriage the passport booklet lists the marriage certificate or the joint photo declaration, and for a major change it lists the newspapers or Gazette document.

Does the Gazette check my documents?

The Gazette prints that no legal responsibility is accepted for notices in that part, and the person notifying remains solely responsible.
