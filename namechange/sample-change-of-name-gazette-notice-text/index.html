What this sample is for

The text of a declaration of a changed name, in the pattern the Gazette notices follow. The Department of Publications’ own form, fee and procedure were not read, so check them before you submit.

Which parts of the Act it follows

  • Gazette of India, Part IV (June 2026 issue): notices read “I hitherto known as … have changed my name and shall hereafter be known as …” and “It is certified that I have complied with other legal requirements in this connection.”
  • The Gazette prints that no legal responsibility is accepted for the publication of these notices and that the person notifying is solely responsible for the legal consequences and any misrepresentation.
  • Passport booklet document 13 and the UIDAI circular both refer to a Gazette notification for a major or complete change of name.

Check before you send

  • Use the same spelling of the old name that appears on your documents.
  • The notice is signed in the existing old name, as the notices we read show.
  • Delete the child paragraph if it does not apply.
  • Check the Department of Publications’ current instructions, form and fee before you submit; we did not read them.

Sources

Read on 14 November 2026: the Passport Seva "Instructions for filling of passport application form" booklet (ApplicationformInstructionBooklet-V3.0.pdf, 21 pages, downloaded from passportindia.gov.in on 14 November 2026), the UIDAI "Standard Operation Procedure (SOP) of Name & Gender Update Request under Exception Handling Process" (a 2021 circular, 6 pages, downloaded from uidai.gov.in on 14 November 2026) and the Gazette of India weekly issue No. 25 of 20 to 26 June 2026, Part IV (egazette.gov.in). We read the passport booklet, the UIDAI circular and one Gazette issue. We did not read the Department of Publications’ procedure or fee for publishing a notice, any State’s rules, or the rules for PAN, banks, voter ID or a driving licence, and the documents lists change, so use the passport portal’s Documents Advisor and UIDAI’s current list before you go.
