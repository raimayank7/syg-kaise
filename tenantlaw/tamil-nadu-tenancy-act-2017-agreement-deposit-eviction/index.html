Tamil Nadu tenancy law: written agreement, deposit and eviction grounds

Tamil Nadu has its own tenancy Act, modelled on the central Model Tenancy Act. This page follows the Gazette text of the Act, the 2018 amendment and the notification that brought it into force.

Sample, not legal advice. This page explains what the Act says in plain words and offers a general sample. It is not advice for your case. Offices, courts, registrars and notaries may ask for their own wording, forms or stamp paper, and the law of your state may add rules. Ask the office concerned or a lawyer before you sign.

Sources

Sources read on 14 November 2026: the Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act, 2017 (Tamil Nadu Act 42 of 2017; Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary of 7 August 2017, from tenancy.tn.gov.in through an Internet Archive copy of 26 January 2025); the Tamil Nadu (Amendment) Act, 2018 (Act 39 of 2018, Gazette of 16 July 2018); and the notification G.O. Ms. No. 35 of 22 February 2019. We read the Act text named on the page, not the rules made under it, the schedule of areas, later amendments or any court judgment. State rent laws are amended; check the current text and your local rent authority or court before you act.

When it came into force

The notification G.O. Ms. No. 35 (Housing and Urban Development), dated 22 February 2019, appoints the 22nd day of February 2019 as the date on which the Act comes into force. We did not read when the 2018 amendment Act came into force or the Rules of 2019.

Written agreement and registration: section 4

Section 4(1) as originally enacted says no person shall, after commencement, let or take on rent any premises except by an agreement in writing, informed jointly to the Rent Authority. The 2018 Amendment Act substituted section 4: every such agreement, and any written agreement already made, shall be registered with the Rent Authority by the landlord or tenant by an application in the First Schedule form within the prescribed time; the Rent Authority shall within thirty days register it and give a registration number, or reject it with written reasons after hearing the parties, and shall upload the parties’ names, premises details and tenure on its website within fifteen days of registration. A new section 4-A says a document required to be registered shall not, unless registered, affect any immovable property in it, confer any power to adopt, or be received in evidence of any transaction affecting that property or conferring any right.

Security deposit: section 11

Section 11(1): save an agreement to the contrary, it shall be unlawful to charge a security deposit in excess of three times the monthly rent. Section 11(2) as enacted: the deposit shall be refunded within one month after vacation of the premises, after due deduction of any liability of the tenant; the 2018 Amendment Act substituted the words so that the refund is due “at the time of taking over of possession of the vacant premises by the landlord”.

When the fixed term ends: section 5(3) as amended

If a fixed-term tenancy ends and is not renewed or the premises are not vacated, and the landlord has not demanded possession of the vacant premises at the end of the tenancy, the tenancy is deemed renewed on a month-to-month basis on the same terms for a maximum period of six months.

If the landlord will not take rent: section 14

Where the landlord does not accept the rent and other charges or refuses to give a receipt, the tenant sends them by postal money order or another prescribed method for two consecutive months, and if the landlord still does not accept them, the tenant shall deposit them with the Rent Authority.

Eviction only by the Rent Court: section 21

A tenant shall not be evicted during the continuance of the tenancy except under section 21(2). The Rent Court may order recovery of possession on listed grounds, including that the landlord and tenant failed to agree the rent under section 8; that the tenant has not paid the arrears of rent and other charges for two months, including interest as agreed or prescribed, within one month of a notice of demand served as section 106(4) of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 provides (no eviction order for non-payment is passed if the tenant pays or deposits all arrears with interest within one month of the notice, but that relief is not available again if the tenant defaults for two consecutive months in any one year after getting it once); that the tenant has parted with possession without the landlord’s written consent; that the tenant has continued misuse of the premises after a notice; and that the landlord requires the premises for repairs or rebuilding that cannot be done without vacating them (with a right of re-entry in the proviso, which we read only in outline). We did not read the remaining grounds.

Common questions

How much deposit can a Tamil Nadu landlord ask?

Section 11(1) of the Act: save an agreement to the contrary, not more than three times the monthly rent.

Does the tenancy agreement have to be registered?

The 2018 Amendment Act’s section 4(3) says every written tenancy agreement shall be registered with the Rent Authority within the prescribed time, and section 4-A says an unregistered one cannot affect the property or be received in evidence of any transaction.

When must the deposit be returned?

As amended in 2018: at the time of taking over possession of the vacant premises by the landlord, after due deduction of any liability.
