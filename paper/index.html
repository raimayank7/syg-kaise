How it works

  1. Open a paper type and set the page size, orientation and spacing.
  2. Watch the sheet redraw below the form.
  3. Press Print and choose "Actual size" (100%), or save the sheet as an SVG.

True size, any printer

Every sheet is drawn in millimetres, so a 5 mm square prints 5 mm wide. A guide on printing at actual size shows the setting that matters. Choose A4, A5, A3, Letter or Legal; the preview shows the paper and the number of squares or staves that fit.

For school, studio and home

Teachers print worksheets and handwriting sheets; students print graph paper for practicals; musicians print blank staves; designers and engineers use isometric and millimetre paper; journal keepers print dot grids. All paper is made by our own code, with no copied artwork.
