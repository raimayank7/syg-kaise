Paper sizes explained

The sizes below are the ones used by the paper makers on this site.

The A series

The A series (ISO 216) is used in India and most of the world. A0 is one square metre. Each next size is half the previous one, folded across the long side. A4 is 210 by 297 mm, A3 is 297 by 420, A5 is 148 by 210 and A6 is 105 by 148. Because every size has the same proportions, a page scales to the next size without distortion.

North American sizes

Letter is 8.5 by 11 inches (215.9 by 279.4 mm) and Legal is 8.5 by 14 inches (215.9 by 355.6 mm). They are used in the United States and Canada. Letter is a little shorter and wider than A4.

B5

B5 (176 by 250 mm) is a common size for notebooks and exercise books in many countries.

Choosing

  • Notes and worksheets: A4 or Letter.
  • Pocket notebooks and journals: A5 or A6.
  • Large plots and posters: A3.
  • Check the tray size in your printer dialog before printing.

How to tell which size you have

Measure the sheet with a ruler. If one side is about 297 mm and the other 210 mm, it is A4. If it is 279 by 216 mm, it is Letter. A sheet of 148 by 210 mm is A5, which is half of A4 folded across its long side, and 420 by 297 mm is A3, two A4 sheets side by side. If your printer asks for a paper size, match the tray: choosing A4 for a Letter sheet makes the page slightly too tall or wide for the sheet, and the printer may scale or crop it. On this site the sheet is drawn at the exact size you pick in the form, so choose the size of the paper in your tray.

Common questions

Can I print Letter paper on an A4 printer?

Yes, if you set the paper size in the print dialog; the page will be scaled or cropped slightly if you leave it on A4.
