Printable Paper

Printable lined paper

Set the line spacing in millimetres, pick the page size and print as many sheets as you like.

Pick the ruling

  • Narrow ruled: about 6.35 mm between lines; small writing, lots of text.
  • College ruled: about 7.1 mm; the usual choice for older students.
  • Wide ruled: about 8.7 mm; for children learning to write.
  • Our own 8 mm default sits between college and wide and suits most adult handwriting.

Why print your own

A printed sheet lets you choose the margin, add a red margin line for teachers' comments, and use up spare office paper. You can also print on thin paper for homework that will be photographed, or on heavier paper for forms. For a plain notebook feel, set the line colour to grey or blue and the weight to the lowest value.

Tips

Print on both sides if your printer can, but check that the lines do not show through badly. Leave the margin line on if the sheets will be punched for a file, as it keeps writing away from the holes.

Paper and pen

Thin paper of 70 to 80 gsm is fine for notes in pencil or ballpoint. For fountain pens or gel pens choose 90 gsm or more and print with a laser printer, as inkjet lines can smudge. A printed margin line is easier to keep tidy than one drawn by hand. Keep extra copies in a folder so you never run out before an exam.

How much fits with these settings

Using the settings this page starts with (margin 12 mm), a sheet holds: A4 portrait: 35 lines; A5 portrait: 24 lines; A3 portrait: 50 lines; Letter portrait: 32 lines. Change the numbers in the form and the preview updates; the line under the preview states the count for your own settings. Landscape swaps the two numbers.

Common questions

Is college ruled the same everywhere?

No. Names and spacings differ between brands and countries. Use the millimetre setting to match the notebook you already have.
