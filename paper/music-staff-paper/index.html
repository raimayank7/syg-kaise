Printable Paper

Printable music staff paper

Blank staves for composing, teaching and practising. Choose the staff size and the gap between staves, then print.

The staff

A staff is five parallel lines. Notes sit on the lines or in the spaces between them. This paper draws the staves with a bar line at each end, ready for you to add a clef, a key signature and bars. The gap between lines is set in millimetres; 2 mm suits normal writing, 1.6 mm gives more staves per page, and 2.5 mm to 3 mm suits beginners and children.

Who it is for

  • Students learning notation.
  • Composers sketching ideas.
  • Teachers making worksheets.
  • Guitarists and singers writing out a melody.

Tip

Print landscape if you write wide phrases. Leave the page portrait for piano or choir parts that need many staves. Use a black line for photocopying; grey lines can vanish on a copier.

Choosing the number of staves

The page fits as many staves as the gap and spacing allow. A bigger staff gap gives fewer, easier-to-read staves, useful for beginners, children and for pieces with many ledger lines. A smaller gap gives more staves per page, useful for long melodies. For a piano piece you will usually need two staves joined together; leave the space between staves wider so that you can draw the brace and the clefs. Print landscape if a line of music needs more bars.

Paper and pen

Print on 90 gsm or heavier paper so pencil erasures do not tear it. Write in pencil first; music has many corrections. Photocopy-friendly black lines work best for parts that will be handed to players. Keep a few sheets with a larger gap for beginners.

How much fits with these settings

Using the settings this page starts with (margin 12 mm), a sheet holds: A4 portrait: 12 staves; A5 portrait: 8 staves; A3 portrait: 18 staves; Letter portrait: 12 staves. Change the numbers in the form and the preview updates; the line under the preview states the count for your own settings. Landscape swaps the two numbers.

Common questions

Does it have a clef?

No, the staves are blank so you can draw any clef. Draw it at the start of each line.
