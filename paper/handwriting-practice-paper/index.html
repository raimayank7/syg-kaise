Printable Paper

Printable handwriting practice paper

Four-line sheets help children and adults keep letter heights equal. Set the size and print.

How the lines work

Each group has four lines. Tall letters reach the top line, small letters fill the space between the dashed midline and the baseline (the blue line), and letters with tails go down to the lowest line. The size you pick is the height of the small-letter band in millimetres: 6 for young children, 4 to 5 for normal writing.

Other scripts

The same lines suit Devanagari and Gurmukhi: the shirorekha (top bar) can sit on the top line or the midline, depending on the school's style. Ask the teacher which guide is wanted and set the spacing accordingly.

Tips for practice

  • Ten minutes a day beats an hour once a week.
  • Trace the first line in each group, then write the rest alone.
  • Check slant and spacing, not only shape.
  • Use a soft pencil for children.

Paper and pen

Print a pack and use one sheet a day. Sit upright with the page slightly tilted and the pen held lightly. Write the same word several times, then compare the first and the last. Praise neat work more than fast work, especially for children.

How much fits with these settings

Using the settings this page starts with (margin 12 mm), a sheet holds: A4 portrait: 10 writing groups; A5 portrait: 7 writing groups; A3 portrait: 15 writing groups; Letter portrait: 9 writing groups. Change the numbers in the form and the preview updates; the line under the preview states the count for your own settings. Landscape swaps the two numbers.

Common questions

Is this suitable for kindergarten?

Use 8 to 10 mm and a darker line for the youngest children; they need bigger guides.
