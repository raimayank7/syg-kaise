Hindi weekly planner (साप्ताहिक योजना)

A weekly planning page with all labels in Hindi.

Labels

Day boxes show the Hindi weekday and the date with the Hindi month name. The two right-hand boxes are labelled मुख्य काम (main tasks) and टिप्पणियाँ (notes). Type a start date for the week, or leave it empty for the current week.

Using it

  • रविवार की शाम को हफ़्ते के तीन सबसे ज़रूरी काम लिखें।
  • पहले तय मुलाक़ातें लिखें, फिर बाक़ी काम।
  • हर शुक्रवार को देखें कि क्या हुआ।

For whom

Hindi-medium students, home managers, shopkeepers and teachers who plan in Hindi. Nobody has proofread these labels with a native speaker beyond the ordinary names of days and months, so tell us through the contact page if you find a mistake.

Using it in a Hindi-speaking home or school

The weekday names and the two box labels are in Hindi, which makes the page easy for children and elders who read Hindi more comfortably than English. A teacher can print one for each class group and ask pupils to write the week's tasks in the day boxes. A family can use the right-hand boxes for market lists and phone calls to make. Type the start date of the week and print; the week begins with the date you give. Anyone who finds a mistake in the Hindi words can tell us through the contact page.

Ideas for adapting it

Print a pack of four to make a month. Use the priorities box for results, not tasks: "finish tax filing" rather than "open the tax website". If you share the planner with a partner or team, write names in the day boxes. Sticky notes on top of the day boxes let you move tasks without rewriting.

Printing this page

The page opens as A4 portrait with a 10 mm margin. Change any of these in the form: A3 gives more writing room, A5 fits a folder, Letter suits North American printers. Press Print and choose Actual size so the sheet matches the preview proportions, or choose Save as PDF to keep it. Print a single test page first; check that nothing is cut at the edge before you print a pack.

Common questions

Can I write in English on it?

Yes. The labels are Hindi, but you can write in any language.
