Printable daily planner

An hour-by-hour page for one day, with priorities and notes beside it.

The schedule

The hourly column runs from the first to the last hour you choose, 6 am to 10 pm by default. Each hour has a ruled row for appointments and tasks. Narrow the range to make rows taller, for example 9 am to 6 pm for an office day.

Priorities and notes

Write at most three priorities. If everything is a priority, nothing is. The notes box catches phone numbers, ideas and things to buy that arrive during the day, so you do not lose them or break your focus.

Habits that help

  • Plan the next day before you finish today.
  • Block time for your hardest task in the part of the day when you are sharpest.
  • Leave gaps between meetings.
  • Cross off finished items; it is satisfying and shows progress.

Ideas for adapting it

Narrow the hours to your real day. A student might use 5 am to 10 pm; an office worker 9 am to 6 pm. Print on both sides to get the notes of the day on the reverse. Keep finished pages in a folder; flipping back shows how long things really took and helps you plan better next time.

Printing this page

The page opens as A4 portrait with a 10 mm margin. Change any of these in the form: A3 gives more writing room, A5 fits a folder, Letter suits North American printers. Press Print and choose Actual size so the sheet matches the preview proportions, or choose Save as PDF to keep it. Print a single test page first; check that nothing is cut at the edge before you print a pack.

Common questions

Can I add the date?

Pick a date in the form and the weekday and date print on the page.
